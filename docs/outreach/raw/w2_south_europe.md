# w2_south_europe: notes

Scope: Italy, Spain (incl. Canarias, Baleares), Portugal, Greece, Malta, Cyprus. Public contacts only, nobody contacted.

## Sources
- **Italy (best source):** ENAC official list of authorised parachuting schools, 2026 edition
  (https://www.enac.gov.it/sicurezza-aerea/paracadutista/elenco-scuole-di-paracadutismo/, XLSX/XML): 33 schools,
  names and towns only. Each school's own site was then opened for email. Aero Club d'Italia contacts page for the federation.
- **Spain:** no public RFAE list of dropzones exists online. Built from each centre's own site, the Manawa Spain guide,
  and Yumping listings (Yumping hides contacts, so those rows are low confidence and carry only the listing URL as contact_form).
- **Portugal:** FPPQ site (fppq.pt) has empty "Entidades filiadas" and "Contactos" links. Schools found from press and their own sites.
- **Greece:** own sites of the three operators (Skydive Athens, Skydive Greece/Megara, Hellenic Skydivers/Skydive Thessaloniki).
- **Cyprus:** British Skydiving drop zone finder (same row as in uk_ie.csv).

## Completeness
- Italy: ENAC lists 33 schools; all 33 are covered (32 rows, Moli-Dzone = FlyGang/Fly4Fun, Montecompatri/San Vincenzo
  likely Fly Zone Roma / Sunflyers). 22 Italian schools have an email from their own site. No contact found for:
  Carabinieri Livorno (military), Crazy Fly Nettuno (domain for sale), Paracadutismo Etruria (blank site), Albatros
  Acqui Terme, Scuola Ferrara, Paraclub Novi Ligure, Fano Sky Team, Area Delta 47 (site under construction),
  Skydive Venice (site, no email). Plus 2 tunnels (Aero Gravity, Fly X), AeCI and ENAC.
- Spain: about 20 active centres found, 13 with email. Missing or weak: Skydive Granada, Pirineos, Ontur/La Mancha,
  Skydive Murcia club, Alicante club (Yumping only). Probably also some small clubs not listed anywhere online.
- Canarias: iJump Gran Canaria is the only active operator found; Skydive Gran Canaria's site is gone (410).
  Tenerife / Lanzarote: none found.
- Baleares: no active dropzone found (skydivemallorca.com is a parked domain).
- Portugal: 6 schools + federation; 4 with email. Skydive Lisboa and Queda Livre could not be reached (may be closed).
- Greece: 3 operators, all with email. Seems complete for active DZs.
- Malta: no dropzone or club found. skydivemalta.com redirects to Skydive Sicilia (noted on that row).
- Cyprus: 1 dropzone (JSPC Cyprus, British Skydiving affiliated).

## Could not reach
- dropzone.com redirects to an unrelated domain; dropzonesandtunnels.com redirects to a betting spam site. Both skipped.
- Many JS-only or blank sites (Etruria, UPnGO, Skydive Venice) show no email; UPnGO has a contact form with an AFF topic.

## Summary
- Rows written: 69
- Rows with email: 46
- Completeness: Italy 33/33 ENAC schools (22 with email); Spain ~20 centres, Portugal 6, Greece 3, Cyprus 1, Malta 0.
- Best source: ENAC 2026 school list (Italy); for the other countries, each school's own site.
- Note: Skydive Lucca has a separate AFF mailbox (aff@paracadutismolucca.it); good fit for the app.

# w2_russia_cis: Russia and CIS (notes)

## Sources used
- Own sites of each club (contacts pages), fetched directly. These give the "high" rows.
- Directories: dropzonefinder.com/dropzones/russia (46 names, no contacts), skydivingsource.com/dropzones/europe/russia/ (22 entries with old sites/phones), skydive-safety.com/Dropzones-Russia.htm (15 old links), parachutist.ru/info/gde-prygnut (43 entries, mostly 2006-2012 data, РОСТО era).
- 2025 VK rating article "Прыжки с парашютом в Москве. Актуальные дропзоны" (vk.ru/@rating_aerotrub-...), mos-holidays.ru list: the best current list for the Moscow area.
- Belarus: ekskursii.by (club names only), dropzonefinder Belarus. Kazakhstan: skydive.kz, Astana Times. Uzbekistan/Kyrgyzstan: search results only.

## Completeness
- 60 rows: 47 Russia, 5 Belarus, 3 Kazakhstan, 2 Uzbekistan, 1 Kyrgyzstan, 1 Armenia, 0 Azerbaijan. 23 with email.
- Moscow area: all 7 DZs in the 2025 rating plus Fly-Zone, Kirzhach aerodrome, Staritsa (good). St Petersburg: 6 working DZs found (Gatchina, DZ Piter, Sky Jumper, Kummolovo, Gorskaya, plus the SPb federation). Regions outside: maybe half of the active DZs; directories list ~46 Russian DZs, I have contacts for ~25 of them.
- Kolchugino: no dropzone found under that name in any source; not in the CSV.
- No creators, podcasts, gear makers or forums collected (search budget ran out before that pass).

## Could not reach
- fpsr.ru refused connections; the ФПСР email in the CSV comes from a 2020 Minsport letter (low).
- Many Russian sites refuse foreign connections or are dead: dztanay.ru / аэродромтанай.рф, sibsky.ru, dzlipetsk.ru, dz-strizh.ru, dzkirzhach.com, skydive-rostov.ru (Tilda 403), dzustinovo.ru (403), aeroclub-gagarin.ru, sky.vvo.ru, mansky.ru (maintenance), fai.org member pages (403).
- Hijacked old domains, do not use: valk.ru (Valkyrie SPb) now redirects to a betting site; dz-volosovo.ru now a casino page (the live Volosovo site is аэродром-волосово.рф); skycenter.ru for sale; skydive.uz lapsed.
- The shared web-search limit for the turn was used up midway, so Armenia/Azerbaijan, Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan got only one search each, and Russian regional searches (Ekaterinburg, Kazan, Krasnodar, Novosibirsk) returned almost nothing useful through the US-only search tool.
- DuckDuckGo shows a CAPTCHA; not used.

## Best single source
- For Moscow: the 2025 VK rating article. For all Russia: skydivingsource.com profiles (old websites + phones) as a starting list, then each club's own site.

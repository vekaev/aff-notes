# w2_dach: Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Liechtenstein

Collected 2026-11-16. Research only: nobody was contacted, no forms were sent, no logins were used.

## Sources
- **Germany: DFV "Plätze & Vereine"**, the alphabetical member directory
  (https://www.dfv.aero/Info/Pl%C3%A4tze%20Vereine/alphabetisch). It has 99 detail pages; one is a test entry
  ("lstest"), so 98 real rows. Each page gives an email, phone, website, contact person, training offered (AFF,
  conventional, tandem) and aircraft. I then opened each club's own homepage plus its kontakt/impressum page. When the
  same email appeared there, the row is marked high. When the DFV lists a personal-provider address (gmx, web.de,
  t-online...) but the club site shows its own info@ address, I used the site's address and put the DFV one in notes.
- **Germany, other**: DFV (association), DAeC, Freifall Xpress (DFV magazine), Airtec/CYPRES, Paratec, and four
  wind-tunnel operators (Indoor Skydiving Bottrop/Viernheim/Leipzig, Hurricane Factory Berlin, Windobona Berlin,
  Jochen Schweizer Arena). The DAeC has no separate skydiving list; skydiving clubs are all under the DFV.
- **Austria: ÖAeC club finder**, filter Sparte = Fallschirm (aeroclub.at/de/sportverband/sektionen/fallschirmspringen/vereine;
  the data comes from the page's own JSON endpoint). It gave 33 clubs. I used only the fields the public page shows
  (name, place, email, homepage, phone). That JSON also holds bank details and private phone numbers; I ignored them.
  Also used: the ÖAeC skydiving section contact page (named role contacts for training, safety and instructors), and
  the commercial operators Bestskydive Tirol, Alpenfreifall, Windobona Wien and Skydiveshop.
- **Switzerland: Swiss Skydive "Schulen/Clubs"** (home.swissskydive.org/schulen-clubs). It lists 13 certified
  schools and about 18 clubs, the clubs with emails. Also used: the Swiss Skydive contact page (office@, formation@,
  safety@), plus Skydive Interlaken, Outdoor Interlaken (AFF to USPA A), EngadinSky and Windwerk (tunnel).
- **Liechtenstein**: no dropzone or club found. Liechtenstein skydivers use Swiss or Austrian dropzones
  (Hohenems/UPCS is the nearest).

## Completeness
- Germany: DFV lists 98 members and I got all 98, plus 9 non-member rows. Some well-known places are not in the DFV
  list and are only partly covered: the Gera dropzone of Aero Fallschirmsport is covered by the skydive.de row, and
  Stade, Laucha, Oppin and similar are missing. Web search was weak for non-member German dropzones; that gap is
  maybe 5–15 sites.
- Austria: all 33 ÖAeC parachute-section clubs, plus 8 others (commercial dropzones, tunnel, shop, federation contacts).
  Six of the "clubs" are airfield or other clubs that only have a parachute section (type=other).
- Switzerland: all 13 certified schools and all 18 listed clubs, plus 4 others. Swiss Skydive's club emails are often
  members' personal addresses; they are kept because the federation publishes them as the contact, and marked medium
  or low.
- Creators and podcasts: none collected in this wave; not searched.

## Could not reach / caveats
- Sites down, or with no email on the page: skydiveswitzerland.ch (down), fsc-paderborn.de and grounded-skydivers.de
  (domain parked), lembergfalken.de (404), fsc-bruchsal.de (503). For these the DFV directory email is used (medium).
- psctriengen.ch and fgf.ch now redirect to Skydive Grenchen, so they may have merged.
- Some DFV rows use a personal-provider address (e.g. hanshelma@web.de, axel_bachert@yahoo.de); the notes say so.
- The Austrian role contacts (Flatz, Schöpf, Löberbauer) are named people at private addresses, but the ÖAeC publishes
  them as section role contacts. For a curriculum fact check, Reinhard Flatz (training/schools) is the right person.

## Best single sources
DFV alphabetical directory (Germany), ÖAeC club finder (Austria), Swiss Skydive Schulen/Clubs (Switzerland).

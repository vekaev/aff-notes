# Australia + New Zealand: notes

## Sources
- APF Dropzone Locator (https://www.apf.com.au/dropzone-locator/dropzone-locator): 37 entries, all types. 16 of them
  are flagged "Training Zone" (noted in `notes`). The APF site blocks plain fetches (403), so it was read in the browser.
- APF Area Councils (https://www.apf.com.au/Sports-Jumping/area-councils): NSWPC, NQPC, NTPC, SAPC, SQPC, VTPC, WAPC.
- VTPC "Victorian Dropzones" page: chief instructors and which DZs are tandem only.
- NZ Skydiving (the NZPIA, now at nzskydiving.org; nzpia.co.nz and nzpf.org redirect there): contact and board pages,
  plus its Google My Map of NZ drop zones (12 placemarks), read as KML.
- Each member's own site (home + /contact) for email and socials. Some sites only answered in the browser.

## Completeness
- Australia: all 37 APF locator entries are in, plus 8 councils/federation and 2 group rows (SKYONE, Skydive Australia).
  Skydive Nagambie and Melbourne Skydive Centre are not in the APF list and their domains no longer resolve. Left out.
- New Zealand: all 12 DZs on the NZ Skydiving map are in, plus Taupo Tandem Skydiving, Skydive Taupo, Skydive Bay of
  Islands, NZ Skydiving School and Ballistic Blondes (now defunct). There is no public NZPIA member list, so this is the
  map plus well-known operators. My guess is about 90% coverage.
- There is no separate "NZPF" for skydiving: nzpf.org.nz is a stamp-collecting federation.

## Could not reach / gaps
- Many AU DZs belong to two groups that share one inbox. SKYONE (Airlie, Cairns, Moruya/Skydive Oz, Sunshine Coast,
  Sydney Skydivers, Tasmania, Torquay) uses info@skyone.com.au. Skydive Australia (10 locations) has a contact form only.
- No email for these: Hillman Farm Skydivers (site needs a login), Kambalda Skysports (no site), Skydiving NSW Drop Zone
  (site 404), Newcastle Sport Parachute Club ("launching soon" page with a form), SAPC (Facebook only), Skydive DropX,
  Blue Skies Matamata.
- The web search budget ran out before I could check GoSkydive NZ, Skydive Kaikoura (domain dead) and a few small NZ
  club fields. Worth a second pass.
- Far North Freefall email comes from the APF locator, not their own site (medium).

## Best source
The APF Dropzone Locator for Australia and the NZ Skydiving DZ map for New Zealand. For AFF, the best targets are the
16 APF training zones, the four AU councils that publish an email, Skydive Auckland/NZ Skydiving School, Taupo Sport
Skydivers and Skydive Abel Tasman.

## Summary
- Rows written: 66 (AU 47, NZ 19)
- Rows with email: 44 (several are shared group inboxes)
- Completeness: AU 37/37 APF DZs + 7/7 councils; NZ 12/12 map DZs + 5 extra
- Best source: APF Dropzone Locator + nzskydiving.org DZ map
- Gaps: Skydive Australia (form only), 4 small WA/NSW DZs with phone only

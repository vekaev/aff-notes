# UK + Ireland: research notes

## Sources
- British Skydiving Drop Zone Locator, https://britishskydiving.org/find-drop-zone/: 24 entries (England, Scotland, NI, Jersey, Isle of Wight, Cyprus). Every one is in the CSV. Emails and phones come from this page (confidence medium) unless a row says otherwise.
- British Skydiving contact page and committees page: HQ, safety mailbox, the Safety & Training Committee (chair, safety officer, secretary), the riggers sub-committee chair and the canopy piloting TEA. TEAs who list only personal hotmail/gmail addresses were left out. The finance and member-development committees were skipped because they don't fit this outreach.
- Own sites, checked: Skydive Ireland, Irish Parachute Club, GoSkydive contact page, Skydive Southwest (high).
- PAI, https://thepai.ie/contact/: only info@thepai.ie and a form. No officers, safety or training contacts are published.

## Completeness
- British Skydiving lists 24 locations. I got all 24, plus 2 GoSkydive sites not in the locator (Old Sarum, Swansea).
- Ireland (Republic): the PAI has had no affiliated drop zones since deregulation, so there is no official list. The Irish Parachute Club (Clonbullogue) is the only active DZ found. The Irish Skydiving Club (Kilkenny) is listed but looks grounded (IAA dispute, Dec 2025). Northern Ireland: Skydive Ireland (Garvagh).
- Wales: the British Skydiving locator has no entry. GoSkydive Swansea is the only Welsh site found.
- I added 6 university clubs as a sample (low confidence, site link only). There is no official list; British University Skydiving / BCPA would hold one, and I didn't reach it.

## Could not reach / caveats
- skydivelocations.com has a bad TLS certificate, so I skipped it.
- Skydive Southwest's email is hidden behind Cloudflare, so the row has a phone only.
- The RAF SPA has no public site or email. The locator's phone is Langar's number, so the charity-register phone was used instead.
- Jersey and Sandown share one phone number in the locator: check it before calling.
- GoSkydive (Headcorn, Old Sarum, Swansea) shows a contact form only. jump@headcorn.com comes from the locator.

## Summary
- Rows written: 41 (26 DZ, 9 association/committee, 6 university clubs)
- Rows with email: 29
- Completeness: all 24 British Skydiving DZs, plus all 2 known Irish DZs (1 active)
- Best source: britishskydiving.org/find-drop-zone/ (the PAI publishes no list)
- Gap: PAI officers and the university clubs list

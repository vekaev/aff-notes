# w2_ukraine_central_east: notes

Scope: Ukraine, Moldova, Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Slovenia, Croatia, Serbia, Bosnia,
Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania, Turkey, Georgia. Public contacts only; no one was contacted, no form submitted.

Parts (merged into `w2_ukraine_central_east.csv`, dedup by name + country) in `parts_w2_ukraine_central_east/`:
`pl_cz_sk_hu.csv` (99), `balkans_ro_bg.csv` (69), `ua_md_tr_ge.csv` (15).

## Sources per country
- Poland: Aeroklub Polski parachute commission (komisja.spadochronowa@aeroklubpolski.pl); dropzone lists on
  wingsuit.cloud, strefaskokow.pl, skydive.pl, plstrefa.pl; each club site. The ULC training-centre register PDF did not load.
- Czechia: firmy.cz "Parachute jumps" category (64 names); Aeroklub ČR parachute commission (para@aecr.cz).
- Slovakia: SNA member list sna.sk/Aerokluby (each club page has email/phone): best and near complete.
- Hungary: MESZ member list skydive.hu/tagszervezetek (17 names, mostly no contacts).
- Romania: aeroclubulromaniei.ro (secretariat + territorial unit emails) plus private DZs.
- Bulgaria: bnac.bg "Структура" member table (contact per club) + caa.bg list.
- Croatia: caf.hr/popis-clanica (email for every club). Serbia: vss.rs/clanovivss (full directory).
- Slovenia: lzs-zveza.si for the federation; DZs one by one. North Macedonia: Aeroklub Skopje. Albania: SkyDive Albania.
- Ukraine: own sites (paraskuf.com.ua, skydive.com.ua), indoorskydiving.world (tunnels), old city guides, Ministry
  of Youth and Sports order naming the federation.
- Moldova: skydive.md (Federația de Parașutism din RM) and its 2025 report PDF (email, Vadul lui Vodă DZ, AFF jumps).
- Turkey: skydiveturkey.com.tr (Antalya, AFF).

## Ukraine: which operate now
Civil skydiving has been halted under martial law since 24 Feb 2022. Para-Skuf (Chayka) and Skydiveua both say so on
their sites, with no newer news; Ukrainian rows are kept as contacts (people, instructors), not active DZs. Wind
tunnels (Aerodium Kyiv, Aerofly) are listed as open. The national federation (ВГО "Федерація парашутного спорту
України") exists in ministry documents but no website or public email was found.

## Completeness
- High: Slovakia, Croatia, Serbia, Bulgaria (full federation directories).
- Medium: Poland (big DZs + ~10 aeroklubs; missing smaller ones e.g. Rybnik, Bielsko, Mielec, Zamość, Stalowa Wola,
  Elbląg, Słupsk), Czechia (~12 clubs name only), Romania, Slovenia.
- Low: Hungary (13 member clubs names only), Ukraine, Moldova, Bosnia (4 rows, no contacts), North Macedonia, Albania,
  Turkey (only Skydive Turkey verified; THK/Türkkuşu contact not verified; Fethiye and other coastal DZs not reached).
- None: Montenegro (no club found, federation domain parked), Georgia (no DZ found; skydive.ge has a broken certificate).

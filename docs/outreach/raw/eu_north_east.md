# eu_north_east: Nordics, Baltics, Central and South-East Europe, Turkey, Ukraine, Moldova, Caucasus, Kazakhstan

Russia and Belarus were skipped, as the brief says. The list was collected on 2026-11-16. Each row was enriched from the organisation's own website: emails, phone and socials.

## What I could not reach
- The shared web-search budget ran out early (the 200-search limit per turn). After that I only probed known or likely domains and read the federation sites directly. This is why Czechia, Slovakia, the Balkans past Serbia, Turkey, Ukraine, Georgia, Cyprus, Iceland and Kazakhstan are thin.
- The dropzone.com, skydivelocations.com and dropzonesandtunnels.com directories were blocked: TLS errors, or a hijacked redirect.
- These pages need JavaScript, so the club lists could not be read: the NIF club list (Norway), SNA Aerokluby (Slovakia), and the club modal links on padobranstvo.com.

## Notes on emails
- Danish chair emails (some are gmail or hotmail) come from the DFU directory: confidence medium. Serbian club emails come from the VSS directory: also medium. Neither was checked on the club's own site.
- Some small clubs publish only a member's personal address on their own site, for example Molde, Lesja, Tromsø and Albatrosz. These are kept, with a note.
- Role addresses useful for this project: kurs@ / elev@ / skolesjef@ (Norway and Sweden student training), ri@sff.se (Swedish chief instructor), lt@laskuvarjotoimikunta.fi (Finnish committee; its student-training leads are named in the row), and komisja.spadochronowa@aeroklubpolski.pl.

## Summary
- Rows written: 186 (171 dropzones/clubs, 12 associations, 2 communities, 1 media).
- Rows with email: 127. Another 3 have a contact form only. Confidence: 110 high, 17 medium, 59 low.
- Completeness: Nordics (minus Iceland), Lithuania, Hungary and Croatia are close to complete. Poland is about 80%. Czechia, Slovakia, Slovenia, the south-east countries, Turkey, Ukraine and the Caucasus/Kazakhstan are thin. Iceland, Cyprus and Georgia are missing.
- Best single source: DFU kluboversigt (all 21 Danish clubs, with chair emails). Next best: MESZ skydive.hu, LPSF members page, and the Aeroklub Polski LKS_2026.pdf.
- Next step if wanted: a follow-up run with a fresh search budget, aimed at Czech, Slovak, Ukrainian, Turkish, Greek, Bulgarian, Icelandic and Georgian dropzones.

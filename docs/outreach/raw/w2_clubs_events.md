# w2_clubs_events: notes

Scope: university clubs, military/veteran teams, communities (women, POPS/SOS, adaptive, LGBTQ+, charity),
boogie/event and competition organisers, national teams.

## Sources used
- POPS national reps directory, https://www.thepops.org/nationaltoppops: 28 country branches, each with a TopPOP email.
  This one source gave the most emails. Note: POPS USA now lists Anne French (anne.toppop@gmail.com) on pops-usa.com.
- BCPA university list (2021 Wayback copy, https://web.archive.org/web/20211019095749/https://www.bcpa.org.uk/Universities):
  about 50 UK university clubs with member counts. bcpa.org.uk is now a hijacked unrelated shop, and buskydiving.org
  does not resolve.
- Students' union and Campus Labs Engage pages (the `/engage/api/discovery/search/organizations` JSON gives the
  club email): Purdue, Georgia Tech, UTK, SDSU, NDSU, UGA, Clemson, NC State, ERAU, Fort Lewis, MIT.
- Official pages: womensskydivingnetwork.org/contact, uspa.org/sis, uspanationals.com, skyleague.com,
  skydivechicago.com/summerfest, britishskydiving.org, ffp.asso.fr, dfv.aero, cspa.ca.
- WebSearch snippets for emails shown on club Facebook pages (Strathclyde, FSU, Texas A&M, QUB). These are marked medium.

## Completeness
- UK university clubs: all 47 BCPA-listed clubs are in. 12 have an email. Many small clubs have only Facebook.
- US collegiate: 26 clubs, 15 with email. USPA has no current public list (its own page says the list is out of date),
  so many clubs are still missing.
- POPS: 28 of 28 national branches.
- Military demo teams (Golden Knights, Leap Frogs, Wings of Blue, RAF Falcons, Red Devils): none publish an email.
  Requests go through forms (DD 2535) or public affairs, and their sites block bots.
- Adaptive, LGBTQ+ and charity groups: names only, no published emails found.
- Not covered: Australian and EU (NL, DE, Nordic) university clubs, Irish clubs other than UL, most national and
  sponsored teams (Arizona Airspeed, Hayabusa, Red Bull), and most boogies (Lost Prairie and others). The shared
  WebSearch limit (200 per turn) ran out partway through, so these need a follow-up run.

## Cautions
- Some emails are student officers' university addresses, published on official campus directories. They change
  every year.
- Hijacked old club domains, do not use: leedsskydivers.com, cuskydiving.com, bcpa.org.uk.

## Summary
- Rows written: 136
- Rows with email: 70 (32 high, 38 medium)
- Completeness: POPS and BCPA UK lists are complete. US collegiate is partial. AU, EU, teams and boogies are thin.
- Best source: the thepops.org national reps page, then the BCPA 2021 archive and Campus Labs JSON.
- Gap: the search limit ran out, so AU/EU clubs, teams and boogies need a follow-up run.

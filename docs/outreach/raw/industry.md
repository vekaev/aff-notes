# Industry contacts: notes

Scope: international bodies and US bodies, gear makers, canopy/AFF coaching, rigger schools, authors, media, insurers,
wind tunnels. Contacts collected only; nobody was contacted.

## Sources
- Each organisation's own contact page (most rows, confidence high).
- USPA staff page (uspa.org/staff): department emails, including safety@uspa.org (Ron Bell, Director of Safety and
  Training) and communications@uspa.org (Parachutist).
- PIA member directory (pia.com/about/our-members): about 190 members, names and countries only. This is the best
  single list for gear makers, lofts and rigging schools. It also lists Kimmel Aviation Insurance, US Academy of
  Parachute Rigging, Mike Turoff and ISIA, all low confidence here.
- Search snippets from manuals or articles for a few emails (Rigging Innovations, L&B, MarS): medium confidence.

## Completeness
- Gear makers: most of the big ones are in (UPT, Sun Path/Javelin, Mirage, Wings, Infinity, Curv, PD, Aerodyne,
  JYRO/Icarus, Fluid Wings, Squirrel, Paratec, Parachutes Australia, SWS, Strong, Cypres, Vigil, Mars, L&B, Alti-2,
  Cookie). Not covered: the rest of the PIA list (military and textile suppliers, small lofts).
- Coaching: Flight-1, PD Factory Team, Alter Ego, Brian Germain, AXIS. No public directory of AFF Course Directors
  was found; USPA does not publish one. Jim Slaton / Skydive University: no public contact.
- Tunnels: iFLY US/UK, SkyVenture (iFLY sales), Paraclete XP, Hurricane Factory, Windoor Realfly, Flyspot.
  Flyspot is the only tunnel that names an AFF-prep package. Bodyflight and Inflight Dubai could not be reached.
- Insurers: only Kimmel, from PIA, with no site found. Weak.
- Not reached: fai.org/IPC (403), squirrel.ws (403), lbaltimeters.com (bot check), chutingstar.com (403),
  bodyflight.co.uk (refused connection), Blue Skies Mag (domain gone), skydiveu.com (403). dropzone.com contact and
  about pages redirect to tanzu.nl, so the owner is unclear.
- National federations (British Skydiving, APF, CSPA) were left to the other agents. The only exception is the
  British Skydiving magazine row, listed as media.
- The search budget ran out partway through. Insurers, rigger schools and authors need another pass.

## Summary
- Rows written: 54
- Rows with email: 33
- Completeness: about 80% of the main gear makers and tunnels, about 50% of coaching orgs, under 20% of insurers,
  rigger schools and authors
- Best single source: the PIA member directory for industry companies, and the USPA staff page for safety and
  Parachutist contacts
- Best first contacts for fact checks: safety@uspa.org and communications@uspa.org

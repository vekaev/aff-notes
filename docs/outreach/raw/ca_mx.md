# Canada, Mexico, Caribbean, Central America: research notes

## Sources
- **CSPA directory** (cspa.ca/en/dropzones, 3 pages; member-type/club and /association). This is the official list. It gives each member's address, website, contact person, email and phone. Emails were decoded from Cloudflare-protected links on the pages.
- **Each dropzone's own website**, fetched once, to confirm the email (marked high when the email appears there).
- **Mexico:** own sites of the Cuautla group (Cuautla, Puerto Escondido, Puebla), Skydive Mexico (Tequesquitengo) and Skydive Playa; for everyone else, the Yumping marketplace (yumping.com/paracaidismo/mexico). Yumping hides operators' own websites and emails, so those rows only have the Yumping page as contact_form.
- **Caribbean and Central America:** web search, then the operator's own site where one exists. Some details come from tourism pages (st-martin.org, martinique.org, serenitrip.fr, varaderoguide).

## Completeness
- **CSPA dropzones:** CSPA lists 27 and I got 27/27. Every one has an email (16 confirmed on their own site, 11 from the directory only). There are 3 more Canadian DZs not on the CSPA list (Skydive Gananoque, Skydive Ontario, Skydive Vancouver/Abbotsford).
- **CSPA associations:** 3 of 3 provincial associations (AB, MB, SK) plus CSPA itself. **CSPA clubs:** 14 of 14 listed. Seven club rows have no email on purpose: the directory gives only a private person's address (telus/shaw/me.com, a student address), so contact_form points to the CSPA page instead.
- **Mexico:** about 30 operators. Only 4 have a verified email (Cuautla, Puerto Escondido, Puebla, Skydive Mexico); most are Yumping listings with no site of their own. **Mexican federation:** no official website or contact found anywhere. The FMP is named only as the channel for FAI records, and ANPAD as the body that certifies instructors. Both rows are low confidence; the best way in is probably to ask Skydive Cuautla or Skydive Mexico.
- **Caribbean:** Puerto Rico, Aruba, Curaçao, Guadeloupe, Martinique, St Martin and Cuba. Nothing found for the Dominican Republic, Jamaica, Bahamas, Cayman, Barbados or Trinidad (the only results were parasailing or paramotor).
- **Central America:** Panama (Chame), Costa Rica (Tamarindo, plus an agency listing for Guanacaste) and Belize (an old tandem/boogie operation). Nothing found for Guatemala, Honduras, El Salvador or Nicaragua.

## Could not reach
- The USPA DZ locator is rendered by JavaScript, so I could not use it to check which foreign DZs are USPA affiliates.
- skydivelocations.com and its API failed the TLS certificate check, so I skipped it. The dropzonesandtunnels.com list is also rendered by JavaScript.
- Mexico's FAI member (the national aero club) and the FMP have no site I could find.

## Flags
- West Edmonton Skydiving Centre is at the same address as the older "Eden North Parachute Schools". The CSPA phone for it has a 902 area code; check it.
- Skydive Cancún (on Yumping) gives the same address as Skydive Playa, so they may be one operator.
- Parachute Montreal Rive-Nord and Rive-Sud share one email.

## Summary
- Rows written: 91 (Canada 48, Mexico 32, Caribbean 7, Central America 4).
- Rows with email: 47.
- Completeness: Canada about 100% of CSPA (27/27 DZs, 14 clubs, 3 associations); Mexico good on major DZs but weak on contacts; Caribbean and Central America sparse because few DZs exist.
- Best source: the CSPA directory (cspa.ca/en/dropzones). Each member page lists contact person, email and phone.
- Weakest area: Mexico's smaller operators and the federation (only reachable through Yumping or by asking the big DZs).

# w2_france_benelux: notes

Scope: France incl. overseas, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Monaco. Collected 2026-11-16. Nobody was contacted.

## Sources
- **FFP "Où pratiquer"** (https://www.ffp.asso.fr/ou-pratiquer/) plus the full list
  (https://www.ffp.asso.fr/ou-pratiquer/liste-de-toutes-les-structures-ffp/). The map embeds JSON for the 49 écoles
  agréées parachutisme, including the technical director (DT) names. Every one of the 165 structure pages
  (`/ou-pratiquer/structures/?code=XXXX`) was read for address, phone, email and site.
- FFP soufflerie map (`?getstructure=Soufflerie`), used for the tunnels' generic emails (iFly, Weembi, FlyZone,
  zerOGravity).
- FFP regional league pages (14 ligues), each with its own contact.
- BE/NL/LU: each centre's own site (homepage or contact page). The lists came from dropzonefinder.com and
  infonu.nl ("10 dropzones"), checked against the live sites.
- Overseas outside FFP: newcaledonia.travel, martinique.org and travel guides (low confidence).

## Completeness
- FFP: all 165 structures listed (49 écoles agréées parachutisme, about 80 clubs, 13 departmental committees,
  parapente/ascensionnel schools), plus the 14 ligues and the federation itself. 11 departmental committees chose
  not to publish contact details, so they have no email. This is effectively complete for FFP-affiliated France,
  including Réunion, Guadeloupe, Tahiti and Monaco (Monaco Parachute Team is an FFP club).
- Not covered: French tandem-only commercial operators that are not FFP-affiliated (there are many small "baptême"
  resellers; most jump at FFP schools anyway).
- Belgium: 6 of 7 DZs per dropzonefinder (Spa, Cerfontaine, Namur/Temploux, Flanders x3 as one row, Antwerp). There
  are also 2 tunnels. The federations (FBP, VVP, the Walloon federation) have no reachable website or email: registry
  entries only.
- Netherlands: Texel, Ameland, Zeeland, Teuge, Hoogeveen, Rotterdam, ENPC, Hilversum, plus Airmoves (now in France),
  the Roosendaal tunnel and KNVvL. Skydive Blauwestad is closed. Stadtlohn is in Germany, so it is left out.
  Probably 9 of about 10 active DZs.
- Luxembourg: the only DZ (Cercle Para, Noertrange) and the LuxFly tunnel (just over the BE border).
- Monaco: no DZ; one FFP club.

## Caveats
- Many FFP club emails are the club officer's own gmail/wanadoo/intradef address, published by FFP as the club
  contact. The notes column flags these. Prefer the school or ligue address where one exists.
- Rows with no email: CEPNC (New Caledonia), Nouméa Skydive (phone and tourism form only), Airmoves, Roosendaal
  tunnel, the BE federations, parachutespringen.info, and the FFP committees that publish nothing.
- Could not reach: basik.fr (gear maker; its email comes from a dealer list), air-colibri-parachutisme.com,
  noumea-skydive sites, cerps.be (domain for sale; the club is now paraclubnamur.be), fwcp.info (now a casino site),
  and api.skydivelocations.com (TLS error). dropzone.com redirects to an unrelated domain.

## Summary
- Rows written: 211 (France 185, Belgium 12, Netherlands 12, Luxembourg 1, Monaco 1)
- Rows with email: 189 (62 dropzones with email)
- Completeness: FFP 165/165 structures plus 14/14 ligues; BE about 6/7 DZs; NL about 9/10; LU 1/1
- Best source: FFP "Où pratiquer" structure pages (one source holds almost all of France and overseas)
- Weakest: Belgian federations and New Caledonia/Martinique (no own-site contacts)

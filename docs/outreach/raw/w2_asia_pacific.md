# w2_asia_pacific: research notes

Scope: India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Philippines, China
(incl. Macau), Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, Mongolia, Pakistan, Fiji / Pacific (Fiji, Tahiti, New
Caledonia, Guam). Australia and NZ left to other agents. Collected 2026-11-16; nobody was contacted.

## Sources used
- Operators' own sites (Thai Sky Adventures, Dropzone Thailand, Skydive Chiang Mai, Skydive Thailand, Skyhigh India,
  Skydive Fujioka, Skydiving Kansai, Fly Station Japan, Skydive Singapore, AltitudeX SG, Skydive Cebu / Palawan /
  Siquijor, Skydive Guam, Noumea Skydive, Everest Skydive, Eagles' Skydive Sri Lanka, MSAF).
- soratobi.link: Japanese list of all skydiving spots with address and phone (best Japan source).
- dropzonefinder.com/dropzones/<country>: names only for China, Japan, Korea (no contacts).
- indoorskydiving.world/country/<country>: wind tunnel lists (names, cities, no contacts).
- skydivephilippines.ph guide, fhta.com.fj (Fiji), tahititourisme.pf, newcaledonia.travel, travel blogs and news for
  the rest.
- YouTube via yt-dlp search (agent-reach's YouTube path; OpenCLI browser bridge was not connected) for creators.

## Completeness (rough)
- Thailand, Singapore, Philippines, Japan, Pacific islands: close to complete for active outdoor DZs.
- India: main commercial DZ (Skyhigh) plus association and older schools. The seasonal Mysuru, Deesa and Dhana camps
  have no named operator or contact online.
- China: weakest. About 6 DZs and 8 tunnels named, almost none with contacts. China has many more bases (Hainan,
  Sichuan and others). Chinese platforms (WeChat, Dianping, Xiaohongshu) hold the real contacts, and I couldn't reach them.
- Korea: Skydive Korea plus a vague "Korea Skydiving School/Academy". Japan: 8 DZs plus 1 tunnel, with no national
  association found.
- Malaysia: MSAF plus Hawk Skydive plus tunnel. Indonesia: tunnels plus a YouTube channel. The FASI parachuting clubs
  (13 civilian/military) are not listed online.
- No civilian dropzone found for Vietnam, Cambodia, Mongolia or Hong Kong (HK Parachute Association appears only in
  old press). Pakistan: only an old press lead.
- The shared web-search budget (200/turn) ran out at the end. Not done: Korea Skydiving Academy contact, Skydive
  Indonesia operator, Thai/Japanese/Korean/Chinese/Taiwanese national associations, more creators and Facebook
  groups.

## Could not reach
- tokyoskydivingclub.jp (403); its email comes from a directory only (low).
- nanki-skydiving.com and skydivefiji.com.fj did not resolve. skydivefast.com is parked.
- dropzonesandtunnels.com redirects to a gambling spam site, so I skipped it. skydivelocations.com has a bad TLS
  certificate. skydiveinasia.com returned 402.
- Skyhigh India's email is hidden by Cloudflare. Use its contact page or phone.

## Best single sources
- Japan: soratobi.link/genre/skydiving-class
- Philippines: skydivephilippines.ph dropzone guide
- Tunnels: indoorskydiving.world country pages
- Strongest fact-check contacts: Skydive Singapore (USPA examiners Danniel Lee, Jack Seet, Randall Koh) and
  Dropzone Thailand / Thai Sky Adventures (full-time USPA AFF DZs).

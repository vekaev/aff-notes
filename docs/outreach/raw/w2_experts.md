# w2_experts: safety experts, paper authors, safety officers

Rows: 66 (61 with an email). Nobody was contacted.

## Sources
- Europe PMC REST API (title/abstract queries on skydiving / parachuting / BASE / wingsuit + injury / fatality / safety; 136 + 49 hits), author affiliation fields, plus open-access full-text XML (103 papers) for corresponding-author emails. Only emails printed in the paper itself were kept; off-topic hits (mitral "parachute" valves, surgical "parachute technique", UAVs) and most military case reports were dropped.
- USPA staff page (uspa.org/staff): department addresses only, no personal emails published.
- PIA committees page (pia.com/about/pia-committees): every chair with a committee address.
- British Skydiving staff page, CSPA staff directory + T&SC page (emails decoded from Cloudflare protection), DFV organigram PDF 2025-2028 (freifallxpress.de), SFF safety presentation PDF (Transportstyrelsen, 2023), FAI ISC T&S Committee report 2024, federation contact pages (NLF, FFP, DFU, KNVvL, PASA, CBPQ, FPSU, Aeroklub Polski, Suomen Ilmailuliitto).
- Brian Germain's own store site (briangermain.company.site) for his email.

## Completeness
- Paper authors: good for the Europe PMC-indexed literature with published emails (~32 people). Papers without an email in the index (e.g. Elsayed 2024 Am Surg, Barrows Australia, Ellitsgaard Denmark) are missing; checking each journal site would add maybe 10-20 more.
- Safety officers: US, UK, Canada, Germany, Sweden, PIA covered well. APF (Australia) blocks fetches: STM Charl Rootman named, no email. NLF (Norway) and FFP (France), DFU, KNVvL, PASA: only general addresses. Swiss, Austrian, Italian, Spanish, Russian national safety officers not reached.
- Parachutist columnists: Safety Check authors have no public emails; the route is communications@uspa.org / safety@uspa.org.
- Book authors: only Brian Germain found with an email. Poynter (died 2015), Kim Emerson / Marcus Antebi (Skydiver's Survival Guide), Flight-1 (form only) not or partly reached.
- The run hit the shared WebSearch limit (200 per turn) midway, so the second half used direct page fetches only.

## Caveats
- Paper emails can be old (pre-2010 ones marked "address dated"). Confidence "medium" for all paper rows: seen in the paper, not on a current staff page.
- SFF ri@sff.se comes from a 2023 SFF slide; the riksinstruktör may have changed.
- Best single sources: Europe PMC (papers) and pia.com committees page (industry safety).

## Summary
- Rows written: 66
- Rows with email: 61
- Completeness: papers ~70% of indexed literature with emails; national safety officers ~10 of ~25 main federations named, fewer with direct emails
- Best source: Europe PMC author affiliations / full texts; PIA committees page
- Gaps: APF, Swiss/Austrian/Italian/Spanish/Russian safety officers, book authors

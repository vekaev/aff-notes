# w2_nordics_baltics: research notes (2026-11-16)

Scope: Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania. Every federation-listed club, plus the tunnels
and gear/rigging businesses found along the way. Nobody was contacted, and no form was submitted.

## Sources (one official list per country, then each club's own site)
- **Sweden:** the SFF club list "Fallskärmsklubbar i Sverige" (16 clubs named; SFF says 17 are active). On 2026-11-16
  sff.se showed only a parked Loopia page, so I read the Wayback copy from 2026-01-25. Please check whether SFF moved
  to a new domain: flygsport.se still links to sff.se.
- **Norway:** the NLF club link points to the NIF directory (minidrett.no, activity 109 = fallskjerm). The pages are
  built by JS, so I read them in the built-in browser. It lists 21 organisations with fallskjerm, plus a "Testklubb" I
  skipped. The email, phone, website and board of each club come from its NIF page.
- **Denmark:** DFU "Kluboversigt" (20 clubs). The emails there are hidden by Cloudflare; I decoded them.
- **Finland:** the Ilmailuliitto club map, filtered to laskuvarjourheilu (16 clubs), plus 2 skydiving businesses from
  "Muut yhteisöt" (Oopee rigging, Parasale gear).
- **Estonia:** the member list of the Estonian Air Sports Federation (lennusport.org/liikmed), 3 parachute clubs.
- **Lithuania:** the LPSF page "Nariai ir narių leidimai" (6 licensed clubs + 2 members without a permit).
- **Latvia:** I found no federation list. The only DZ found is Skydive Latvia (Langači, near Limbaži). I also added the
  Aerodium Sigulda tunnel.
- **Iceland:** no club website found. I added the federation name (Fallhlífasamband Íslands, from a 2007 city
  grants PDF) and Skydive Iceland, Hella. Skydive Mag reports Hella has been inactive for about 5 years. Low confidence.

## Completeness
- Sweden 15/16 SFF clubs + Bodyflight tunnel. Norway 21/21 NIF clubs + VossVind tunnel. Denmark 20/20 DFU clubs.
  Finland 16/16 SIL clubs. Lithuania 8/8 LPSF members. Estonia 3/3 ELF parachute members. Latvia and Iceland: best
  effort, likely complete for active DZs but unconfirmed.
- 97 rows, 62 with an email.

## Choices and gaps
- **Personal emails left out on purpose:** when a directory gave only a chair's private address (gmail/hotmail with a
  person's name), I left email empty and said so in notes. This hit Føniks, Gudvangen, Lesja, Lofoten, Veteranenes,
  NFK, Sydsjælland, ØFK, POPS-DK and Kauno PK. Role mailboxes (formand@, leder@, club gmail) are kept.
- **For student contact I picked the student/course mailbox** where a club had one (elev@, solo@, kurs@, kurssit@,
  skolesjef@), and put the general address in notes.
- **No email found** (JS sites or contact form only): Umeå, Söderhamn, Gefle CReW, Gotland, Skåne (SE); Pori, Skydive
  Finland/Utti, Tampere, KLU, Kemi (FI); Mažeikiai (LT); Aerodium; ELF. These rows carry social links.
- **Sites that did not load:** idrottonline (Gefle), skydiveskane.se, klu.fi, øfk.dk, skydive.puslapiai.lt,
  skydiving.ee.
- dropzone.com/dropzones redirects to an unrelated domain, and dropzonesandtunnels.com listings redirect to a gambling
  site. Neither was used.

## Best single sources
NIF/minidrett (Norway: emails, phones and boards in one place) and the DFU Kluboversigt (Denmark). For the region
overall, the national federation lists cover nearly everything; no single cross-country list exists.

## Summary
- Rows written: 97 (SE 17, NO 23, DK 21, FI 19, LT 9, EE 4, LV 2, IS 2)
- Rows with email: 62
- Completeness: all federation-listed clubs in SE/NO/DK/FI/LT/EE. Latvia and Iceland are thin: no federation list found.
- Best source: NIF minidrett directory (NO) and DFU Kluboversigt (DK). For Sweden, the archived SFF list (sff.se parked).
- Not reachable: Iceland club contacts, Latvian federation, and ~14 clubs with no published email (social links given).

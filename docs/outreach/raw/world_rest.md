# world_rest.csv: notes

Scope: dropzones, schools, wind tunnels and national federations in South America, Africa, the Middle East
(incl. Turkey, Iran) and Asia. Collected 2026-11-16. Nobody was contacted; no forms were submitted.

## Sources
- **Brazil: CBPq** (cbpq.org.br/filiados/clubes/ and jump-area list): 135 clubs, 41 jump areas, 21 state
  federations, each with email + phone. The best single source in this file (~185 rows).
- **Argentina: FAP** (paracaidismo.org.ar) affiliates + the official ANAC list of licensed schools (PDF).
- **Colombia: Fedeaereos** (fedeaereos.org) skydiving clubs.
- **South Africa: PASA** directory (para.co.za/DropZones, details per dropzone id): 14 current dropzones and
  15 older entries marked low (some may be closed).
- **Tunnels: indoorskydivingsource.com/tunnels/** (directory page). Most tunnel rows have only this source
  and are marked low. Military-only tunnels are left out.
- Operators' own websites (homepage + /contact) for everything else.

## Completeness
- Brazil ~95% of CBPq; Argentina and Colombia complete against their federation lists (many Argentine clubs
  publish only Instagram/WhatsApp).
- South Africa ~95% of PASA.
- Chile, Peru, Uruguay, Paraguay, Bolivia, Venezuela: partial (40-70%). Ecuador: none found.
- Rest of Africa ~50-60% (Namibia, Kenya, Tanzania, Egypt, Morocco found). Not searched: Mauritius, Reunion,
  Tunisia, Botswana, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Ghana, Nigeria, Uganda; Egyptian/Moroccan federations.
- Middle East and Asia: thin (~30-40%). The shared web-search budget (200 per turn) ran out before these
  regions, so they were built only from known operator domains and the tunnel directory. Missing for sure:
  most Japanese dropzones (Fujikawa, Okinawa, etc.), Chinese dropzones and the Chinese/Indian/Thai/UAE/Saudi/
  Israeli/Jordanian federations, Skydive Abu Dhabi, Al Hamra (RAK), Oman, Philippines (Pampanga), Indian
  military-run AFF courses, Central Asia beyond Astana, Fethiye/Didim in Turkey, Cyprus, Lebanon.

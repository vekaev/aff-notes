# Glossary: Chinese, Simplified (zh), mainland China

Terms as used in mainland Chinese sport parachuting (中国航空运动协会 / ASFC, 跳伞 clubs and the parachute
industry) and in Chinese paragliding material for canopy-control words (组带, 操纵绳, 刹车), which mainland
skydivers share. Where Chinese jumpers keep the English word (AFF, AAD, RSL, PLF, hard deck, lock on, slider,
the exit-count calls), the app keeps it and adds the Chinese word in brackets once.

Taiwan / Hong Kong use Traditional script and some different words; they are noted in the last column for a
future `zh-Hant` file. Western digits and `ft`, `s`, `m`, `mph` are kept; thousands use a comma (5,500 ft). No
Chinese-character numerals replace digits; numbers written as words in English ("two tries") stay words (两次).

Sources:
- 降落伞 (zh.wikipedia): parts list 伞衣, 引导伞, 伞绳, 背带系统, 伞包, 备份伞, 引张带 (static line):
  https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/降落伞
- 跳伞 (zh.wikipedia), general terms 跳伞, 降落伞, 特技跳伞: https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/跳伞
- 中国航空运动协会 (ASFC), national body for 跳伞 and 滑翔伞: https://www.asfc.org.cn/
- USPA SIM 4-1 (English source of every procedure in the app): https://www.uspa.org/sim/4-1
- Not found online from here (search is US-only): an ASFC AFF training manual or Chinese exam questions. Terms
  below marked "check" are the common club usage I am least sure of; a native instructor should confirm them.

Choices worth knowing:
- The AI prompt (`108z8a3`) ends "请用中文回答。" (English: "Reply in English."), localised like German.
- `English` (language name) kept as is.
- Month names written in characters (五月, 一至五月) so no digit is added that the English does not have.
- Percent strings that start mid-sentence in the app (`3ss39a`, `8vx71x`, `h9xb21`) begin with 的 so they read
  after the big number ("XX% 的伤害……").
- "6.2 M jumps" kept as "6.2 M 次跳伞" because the number check needs the same digits (620 万 would fail).

# Glossary: Japanese (ja), Japan

Japanese skydiving keeps most gear and procedure words as katakana loanwords (メインキャノピー, リザーブ, カッタウェイ,
フレア, ランディングパターン...). This matches how Japanese dropzones and the American-trained AFF instructors who
teach there talk. Where a plain Japanese word exists and helps (異常開傘 for malfunction, パラシュート着地法 for PLF,
決心高度 for decision altitude), it is used once next to the loanword or as the main term. Numbers: Western digits;
English number words ("two tries", "one finger") become kanji numerals (二回, 指一本) so the app's number test keeps
matching the English. Units ft / s / m / mph / in stay as in English.

Sources and their limits:
- I could not find a public Japanese AFF manual or exam-question set from the Japanese federations
  (日本パラシューティング連盟 / 日本スカイダイビング協会: I could not find either one's official site or any public
  training material by web search, so no link is given; the FAI Skydiving Commission lists national members:
  https://fai.org/node/24627). Web searches in Japanese for the glossary terms returned only English
  USPA material. The terms below are therefore the katakana loanwords in common use at Japanese dropzones and in
  Japanese skydiving blogs, plus standard aviation Japanese (決心高度, 風上 / 風下, 吹き流し). **Every term should be
  checked by a Japanese AFF instructor** (see "least sure" below).
- USPA SIM 4-1 (the English source of the procedures): https://uspa.org/sim/4-1
- Wikipedia (ja) "スカイダイビング" for general vocabulary: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/スカイダイビング

## The 5 terms I was least sure about (for a native instructor)

1. **緊急処置** for "emergency procedure": some schools may say 緊急操作 or 緊急手順, or just "EP".
2. **PLF（パラシュート着地法）**: the Japanese name in use may be 五点着地 / 五点着地法 (from military parachuting);
   I kept "PLF" as the main word.
3. **トグルをセットしたまま / トグルを外す** for "brakes stowed / unstow the toggles": unsure which verb Japanese
   instructors use (ブレーキを外す, リリースする).
4. **ホールディングポイント / ホールディングエリア** for the holding area: may be called 待機エリア at some dropzones.
5. **決心高度** for "decision altitude" (borrowed from aviation): skydivers may just say ディシジョンアルティテュード
   or 判断高度.

Drift found and fixed during the check:
- Early drafts used digits for English number words ("2回試す" for "two tries", "指1本" for "one finger",
  "5月" in "Mid-May 2019"). The number test flagged them; changed to kanji numerals (二回, 指一本, 五月) so no number
  is added. Meaning unchanged.
- Two strings had the bold split differently from English (`<b>AADが</b>…<b>作動させた</b>`); merged into one bold
  span to match the English tag count.
- "Jan–May 2025" is written 2025年の一月から五月 for the same reason.
- "brakes" in "Releasing the brakes while still twisted" was kept as ブレーキを外す (unstow), not ブレーキをかける
  (apply), which would invert the meaning.

# Glosar românesc (ro) - AFF notes

Terms as used by Romanian parachuting (Aeroclubul României, the Romanian Civil Aeronautical Authority AACR / RoCAA
regulations, Romanian dropzones). Romanian skydivers keep many English words (AFF, cutaway, flare, slider, RSL, AAD,
box, wave-off, spot, dropzone); the app keeps them and adds the Romanian word in brackets once where useful.
Informal "tu" address, as Romanian instructors use with students.

## Least sure (for a native instructor)
1. **larguare / a larga** for cutaway: from the Aeroclub quote "a largat"; some Romanian jumpers may simply say "cutaway" or "desprindere".
2. **parașuta extractoare** for pilot chute (vs "pilot chute" kept in English, or "extractor").
3. **comenzi** for toggles and **chingile din spate** for rear risers (some say "toggle-uri", "risere").
4. **arcuire / arcuiește-te** for arch (vs keeping "arch").
5. **poziționarea în ușă** for climb-out and **limita minimă (hard deck)**.

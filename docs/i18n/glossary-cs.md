# Glossary: Czech (cs)

Terms as used in Czech parachuting: the ÚCL (Czech Civil Aviation Authority) parachuting regulation V-PARA-1, which
LAA ČR and Czech parachute schools work under, Czech incident reports and common dropzone usage. Czech skydivers keep
many English words (AFF, slider, RSL, AAD, hard deck, lock on, downplane, PLF, box); where they do, the app keeps the
English word and adds the Czech one in brackets once where it helps. The app addresses the student with the informal
"ty", like Czech dropzones do.

Sources:
- ÚCL, V-PARA-1 "Předpis pro provádění seskoků padákem v ČR", 6th edition, 2026 (definitions: záložní padák,
  výtažný padáček, odhoz hlavního padáku, automatický zabezpečovací přístroj AAD, spřažený odhoz RSL, vrchlík,
  výška otevření padáku, nosný postroj s obalem padáku, pracovní nálet letadla, prsní poloha, H-AFF "instruktor
  zrychleného výcviku volných pádů", wind limits in m/s):
  https://www.caa.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/1453-26-301-V-PARA-1-6.-vydani.pdf
- ÚCL incident reports on cutaways, line twists and reserve openings (odhoz, zakroucení, záložní padák, řídicí
  šňůra): https://www.caa.cz/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Incident-28.5.2016-3.pdf ,
  https://caa.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/V%C3%A1%C5%BEn%C3%BD-Incident-Ji%C4%8D%C3%ADn-13.10.pdf
- Czech patent CS248893B1 (reserve opening after release of both "volné konce" = risers):
  https://patents.google.com/patent/CS248893B1/cs
- AFF course descriptions from Czech dropzones (e.g. Adrop Příbram): https://www.adrop.cz/en/parachute-training-free-fall
- USPA SIM glossary (English reference): https://uspa.org/SIM/Glossary

Drift found and fixed during the review:
- "C-1: first release" was first drafted as "first solo jump" (wrong meaning: C-1 is the first time the instructors
  let go); fixed to "první puštění".
- "Spinning and nearly no pull" (a malfunction-type label) was first drafted as "spins and barely brakes"; fixed to
  "Rotace a téměř neotevřený" to match the other languages (Spanish/Ukrainian "almost not opened").
- "AFF graduation jump here (2015)" was first drafted with "my"; the possessive was dropped.
- The count "one thousand, two thousand" was rewritten in the natural Czech order "tisíc jedna, tisíc dva".

Least sure (for a native instructor to check): "podrovnání" for flare (some Czech dropzones simply say "flare"),
"rukojeť odhozu / polštářek odhozu" for the cutaway handle, "příčný úsek (base)" for the base leg, "cvičné dosahy"
for practice touches, and "kruh pozornosti" for circle of awareness.

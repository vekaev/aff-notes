# Glossary: Bulgarian (bg)

Bulgarian AFF schools (Skydive Bulgaria, Skydive Sofia, Sky Adventures, Летателен център АВА) teach the USPA
programme, often with USPA-rated instructors, and many words stay in English in daily use (AFF, slider, flare, RSL,
AAD, box, wave-off, lock on, pin check, „Check in / Check out / Prop / Up / Down / Arch“). Older Bulgarian parachuting
(military / ДОСО tradition) shares the Russian vocabulary (купол, раница, подвесна система, откачане, запасен
парашут). The app uses the plain Bulgarian word where one exists and keeps the English one in brackets once where
students will hear it at the dropzone.

No public Bulgarian federation training manual or exam bank was found online; terms marked "by usage" should be
confirmed by a Bulgarian instructor.

## Least sure (for a Bulgarian instructor)

1. toggles → „тогли / тогъл“ (vs „клеванти“, „дръжки за управление“).
2. rear risers → „задни ремъци“ (vs „задни свободни краища“, „райзъри“).
3. reserve → „резерва“ / „резервен купол“ (vs „запасен парашут“).
4. cutaway → „откачване / откачи“ (vs „откачане“, or the English "cutaway").
5. lines → „линии“ (vs „стропи“).

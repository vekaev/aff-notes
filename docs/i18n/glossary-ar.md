# Arabic (ar) glossary: AFF notes

Modern Standard Arabic, Western digits only (0-9), no direction marks (the app sets right-to-left).

## How Arabic skydiving vocabulary works (read first)

- Gulf dropzones (Skydive Dubai, Skydive Abu Dhabi/Al Jazira Aviation Club, Saudi and Qatari schools) teach AFF in
  **English** under USPA rules; students hear "cutaway", "flare", "AFF", "AAD" in English. No public Arabic federation
  manual with a full AFF vocabulary was found online (searches in Arabic returned only manufacturer pages, e.g.
  Garmin's Arabic manuals, and English sources).
- Arab armed forces' parachuting (military and civil paratroop training) uses a stable MSA core: مظلة (parachute /
  canopy), المظلة الرئيسية, المظلة الاحتياطية, القفز الحر / السقوط الحر, مقياس الارتفاع, الهبوط, منطقة الهبوط.
- Rule used: the Arabic word first, and where students will hear the English word on the dropzone, the English term
  once in brackets the first time it matters, e.g. "مقبض الفصل (cutaway)". Acronyms (AFF, AAD, RSL, MARD, PRO, PLF,
  USPA, SIM) stay in Latin letters.

Sources:
- Skydive Dubai AFF course (taught in English, USPA): https://skydivedubai.ae/school/aaf-course
- Skydive Dubai USPA A licence: https://www.skydivedubai.ae/school/uspa-a-license
- USPA SIM glossary (English source terms): https://uspa.org/SIM/Glossary
- Garmin Arabic manual, "محترف القفز" (jumpmaster) feature, for Arabic altitude / release-point wording:
  https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-3A4F9C4A-8735-46C0-8DA9-65F11400B150/AR-AE/GUID-97DD3FB0-DB77-4771-A1EE-C86E1901805B.html
- General Arabic military-parachuting usage (paratroopers, المظليون) from Arabic press and training descriptions;
  not a single citable manual. Terms marked "check" below need a native instructor.

# Glossary: Serbian, Latin script (sr)

Ekavian Serbian, Latin script, informal "ti" like the English "you". Serbian sport parachuting (Vazduhoplovni savez
Srbije, Serbian clubs, the Serbian Army's 63rd Parachute Brigade) has very little civilian AFF material published
online. Terms below come from the closest written regulations in the same language (Croatian Pravilnik o
padobranstvu, which uses the shared aviation vocabulary) adapted to Serbian ekavian, plus common club usage. Where
the sport keeps the English word (AFF, slider, PLF, RSL, AAD, downplane, biplane, line-over, wave-off, lock on,
hackey) the app keeps it, with a Serbian gloss in brackets the first time it helps.

Sources:
- Pravilnik o padobranstvu (NN 65/2015), definitions: glavni padobran, rezervni padobran ("u slučaju otkaza glavnog
  padobrana"), padobranski automat (AAD), RSL ("veza između slobodnih krajeva glavnog padobrana i sistema za
  aktiviranje rezervnog padobrana"), sistem veza, aktiviranje padobrana, skok na slobodno, zadrška, doskočište:
  https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2015_06_65_1252.html
- Croatian CAA AFF definitions (AFF nastavnik, rezervni padobran, otkaz): https://www.ccaa.hr/file/891483dfad34b2b32ba74365165c0577f51
- Serbian Ministry of Defence, free-fall parachute training articles (skokovi na slobodno, padobran-krilo):
  https://www.mod.gov.rs/eng/22454/napredna-obuka-padobranaca-za-skokove-na-slobodno-dejstvo
- Montenegro, AFF instructor authorisation (AFF instruktor padobranstva):
  https://psc.gov.me/en/sektorske-informacije/ministarstvo-saobracaja-i-pomorstva/sticanje-ovlascenja-aff-instruktora-padobranstva/

Drift found and fixed:
- "finish as you sink" was first written "tonješ" (wrong verb form); fixed to "toneš" everywhere (7 strings).
- "Stay in until you are in the water, then swim away": typo "otpivaj" fixed to "otplivaj".
- "No, it's below 2,500 ft" first read "Ne, ispod je 2.500 ft" (ambiguous: "below is 2,500 ft"); fixed to
  "Ne, to je ispod 2.500 ft".
- "flare from those brakes" kept literal as "koči iz tog položaja kočnica" (flare from that brake position), not
  "koči odmah", so the meaning "don't let up first" stays.
- "rear risers" uses "zadnji slobodni krajevi" consistently (Pravilnik term), never "zadnje trake", so a student does
  not confuse risers with lines ("konopci").

## Least sure (for a native instructor to check)

1. **flare = "kočenje"** (with "flare" kept once): Serbian jumpers may say "flerovanje" or "kočenje"; "kočenje" also
   means braking generally (brakes set = "kočnice").
2. **rear risers = "zadnji slobodni krajevi"**: regulation term; clubs may say "zadnje trake" or "rajzeri".
3. **canopy = "kupola"** vs "krilo": older round-canopy word; ram-air jumpers may prefer "krilo" or "padobran".
4. **pilot chute = "izvlačni padobran"** (and hackey = "loptica"): clubs may say "pilot", "pilotić" or "mali padobran".
5. **hard deck = "donja granica"** and **decision altitude = "visina odluke"**: no published Serbian AFF syllabus
   found to confirm.

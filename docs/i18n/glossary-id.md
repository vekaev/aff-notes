# Glossary: Indonesian (id), Indonesia / Malaysia

Terms as used in Indonesian sport parachuting (terjun payung; FASI, Federasi Aero Sport Indonesia, and Persatuan
Terjun Payung clubs) and readable in Malaysia. Public Indonesian training material for sport AFF is very thin: no
FASI student manual or exam bank was found online. Indonesian sport jumpers and instructors (many trained abroad
or at Bali / Java dropzones with foreign staff) mostly keep the English words for gear and procedures (cutaway,
flare, slider, toggle, pilot chute, AFF, AAD, RSL, PLF, hard deck). The app keeps those words and uses plain
Indonesian for everything else (ketinggian, parasut utama / cadangan, tali, pendaratan, prosedur darurat).
Informal "kamu" is used for the reader (study notes for a student), "Anda" in the questions to instructors.

Sources:
- Indonesian Wikipedia, "Terjun payung" (parasut, kanopi, harness, ransel, ketinggian, mendarat, terjun bebas): https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terjun_payung
- FASI (Federasi Aero Sport Indonesia), parent body for terjun payung (site not opened; no public student manual found): https://fasi.or.id
- USPA SIM 4-1 (the English source text the app follows): https://uspa.org/sim/4-1
- General Indonesian aviation words (lepas landas, baling-baling, pesawat, landasan) as in KBBI: https://kbbi.kemdikbud.go.id

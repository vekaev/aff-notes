# Glossary: Norwegian Bokmål (`nb`)

Terms follow the usage of Norges Luftsportforbund, Fallskjermseksjonen (F/NLF) where a source was found. Where
Norwegian jumpers keep the English word (lock on, wave-off, break-off, downplane, line-over, slider, flare, PLF,
circle of awareness, hard deck), the app keeps it too, and adds the Norwegian word in brackets once where it helps.
Entries marked **(unverified)** were not found in an F/NLF document and need a Norwegian instructor's check.

### Drift found and fixed

- **"backup" vs "reserve"**: the first draft rendered "a backup, not a plan" (AAD, RSL, radio) as *en reserve*,
  which a student could read as "the reserve canopy". Changed everywhere to *en sikring* (a safeguard).
- **9bz4av** "Then open the reserve": first draft split the bold over two words (*Åpne* … *reserven*), which broke
  the tag check; rewritten as "Så **åpner du reserven**".
- **1k37gkf** "turn your head": first draft added "away" (*vri hodet bort*); changed to *vri på hodet* to add nothing.
- **"the five-second rule"** in a link title was written with a digit, which added a number; changed to
  *femsekundersregelen*.
- **EP abbreviation**: kept "EP" with *nødprosedyre (emergency procedure)* instead of inventing "NP".
- **"right of way"** (lower canopy): first draft was awkward; now *har forkjørsrett*.
- **Prompt for AI (108z8a3)**: "Reply in English" becomes "Svar på norsk", the same intent as the app's own
  per-language replacement.
- Order of actions in every emergency procedure was compared line by line with English: unchanged.

## Least sure (for a Norwegian instructor)

1. **svai** for "arch" (vs keeping "arch").
2. **kuttpute** for the red cutaway handle (F/NLF uses it for the pillow handle; is it also said for a loop handle?).
3. **styrehåndtak** for toggles and **bakre bærestropper** for rear risers.
4. **linetvinn** for line twists, and **prøvetrekk** for practice touches.
5. **venteområde / ventepunkt** for holding area / point, and **materiellkontrollør (rigger)** for the rigger.

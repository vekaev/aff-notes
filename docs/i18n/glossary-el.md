# Greek glossary (el) - AFF notes

Terms for Greece and Cyprus. Greek sport skydiving schools work under the Hellenic Air Sports Federation (ΕΛΑΟ) and
the "Κανονισμός Σχολών Ερασιτεχνών Αλεξιπτωτισμού" (ΦΕΚ 1347/2000); most instructors trained abroad (USPA, British
Skydiving) and keep many English words on the dropzone (cutaway, flare, slider, hackey, pilot chute, spot, wave-off).
Where the sport keeps the English word, the app keeps it and adds the Greek word in brackets once where it helps.
Formal Greek terms (θόλος, κύριο / εφεδρικό, αυτόματος μηχανισμός, φρένα, ζώνη ρίψης, συσκευαστής) are taken from the
official Greek accident report on a sport-skydiving accident at Kopaida (ΕΔΑΑΠ).

The app speaks to the student in the informal singular (εσύ), short imperatives, like the English.

### Drift found and fixed
- `tdzub9`: "diagonally across it" was shortened to "diagonally"; restored ("διαγώνια κατά μήκος της").
- `19o7hwy`: "steerable" was rendered once as "ελέγχεται"; unified with the canopy-check word "κατευθυνόμενος" used everywhere else.
- "pump the toggles" had two renderings across chunks («πάμπαρε» and "κάνε pumping"); unified to "κάνε pumping με τα χειριστήρια".
- "Rock" in the exit count unified to "λίκνισμα (rock)"; "brace" unified to "θέση πρόσκρουσης"; "EP" = ΔΕΑ (Διαδικασία Έκτακτης Ανάγκης).
- Grammar: "κύριο σου" → "κύριό σου"; "στον αεροδυναμικό σήραγγα" → "στην αεροδυναμική σήραγγα".
- Order of actions in every emergency procedure was checked against English: unchanged.

## For a native instructor to check
1. Cutaway as "αποκοπή / κάνε αποκοπή": whether Greek dropzones say "cutaway" in English instead.
2. PLF gloss "προσγείωση με κύλιση" (military may say "ανατροπή").
3. Canopy check words "υπάρχει, τετράγωνος, σταθερός, κατευθυνόμενος" (dropzones may keep "there, square, stable, steerable").
4. "τόξο" for arch and the hand-signal words (πόδια έξω, χαλάρωσε, έλεγξε ύψος, άνοιξε).
5. "κάνε pumping με τα χειριστήρια" for "pump the toggles" and "σημείο αναμονής" for holding point.

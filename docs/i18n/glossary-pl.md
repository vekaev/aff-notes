# Glossary: Polish (pl)

Terms as used in Polish skydiving schools (Aeroklub Polski / Polish dropzones) and Polish aviation documents. Many
Polish skydivers keep English words (AFF, slider, flare, hackey, cutaway, RSL, AAD, box); where they do, the Polish
word is given once in brackets in the app text.

Sources:
- ULC (Polish Civil Aviation Authority), "Skoki spadochronowe – materiał doradczy":
  https://ulc.gov.pl/_download/bezpieczenstow_lotow/materia%C5%82y_sms/Skoki_spadochronowe_materia%C5%82_doradczy_v1.pdf
  (czasza główna, spadochron zapasowy, pokrowiec, taśmy nośne, zawleczki, linki, wysokość otwarcia, awarie przy dużej
  prędkości / przy małej prędkości, strefa zrzutu, miejsce lądowania)
- PARP course cards "Skoki spadochronowe dla początkujących AFF-6" (Polish AFF course syllabus: czasza główna,
  czasza zapasowa, uprząż, AAD, procedury awaryjne): https://uslugirozwojowe.parp.gov.pl/wyszukiwarka/uslugi/drukuj-pdf?id=2877766
- ULC circular on aerodrome traffic circuit legs (z wiatrem, po pierwszym/trawers, prosta):
  https://pkbwl.gov.pl/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/2020_3377_okolnik_doradczy_ULC.pdf
- USPA SIM glossary (English reference): https://uspa.org/SIM/Glossary
- Common Polish dropzone usage (Skydive Kraków, Skydive Warszawa, Aeroklub forums): wyczepienie, pilocik, sterówki,
  skrętka linek, zapas, AAD/Cypres.

## Back-translation

30 of the most safety-critical strings, Polish → English by the translator (key: back-translation). Meaning and order
of actions match the English in all 30.

1. `104so7e` Tangled canopies: don't cut away. Free what you can, pull in the less-filled canopy hand over hand and do a PLF (USPA SIM 4-1).
2. `107azwz` A level wing is more important than the perfect spot. Landing priorities: 1 level wing, flying straight; 2 clear terrain, even farther away; 3 flare and PLF.
3. `11bwry5` Two tries or 5 s, whichever comes first, then the emergency procedure. USPA SIM says reserve; ask whether your school teaches reserve only or a cutaway first.
4. `11c6qqs` Right hand to the pilot chute handle (small "ball" at the bottom of the container, on the right), left hand above the head for balance. Pull it out and throw it to the side at arm's length, then back to the arch and count. Can't find it or it won't come out? Two tries or 5 s, then Part IV.
5. `11owloq` A spin gets worse fast: decide, cut away, reserve.
6. `11xnfod` Already in a low turn? Opposite toggle down to stop the turn and bring the canopy back overhead. Then flare from that braking and prepare for a hard landing (PLF). Hit earlier? Keep flying the canopy.
7. `12ns35n` Over 4000 cutaways a year in the US, reserves rarely fail: delay kills. Unclear canopy? At most 5 s to see it clearly improving; if not, cut away and open the reserve. Line twists with rotation: cut away immediately. As a student never below 2500 ft.
8. `14pdpw7` Unclear canopy: at most 5 s to decide, counting out loud. It's a limit, not waiting.
9. `154vw5m` Your main canopy is bad at 2100 ft. Can you still cut away?
10. `15abr6m` below it you don't cut away: you open the reserve only (one exception: downplane).
11. `15pbnlv` Emergency procedure. USPA allows two ways: reserve only, or cutaway then reserve. Do what your school teaches.
12. `16yhndd` In water: cut away, arms back, swim forward out of the harness.
13. `18qw6t8` Fixable, but only two tries: not fixed by 2500 ft → emergency procedure →
14. `19au8ek` 300 ft. Below it only small flat corrections, up to 45°. ... Above 1000 ft: up to 180°. 1000 → 300 ft: up to 90°.
15. `1b49w3n` About 10 ft (3 m) above the ground. Too high: don't let the toggles up. Stop, hold what you have, fly straight, finish as you sink and prepare for PLF.
16. `1b5grfd` 2500 ft is when you should already have decided; 1000 ft is the lowest you cut away. A jumper with over 1200 jumps confused this and didn't cut away at 2100 ft.
17. `1g4aj2u` Below the hard deck a cutaway leaves too little height for the reserve. Pull silver and land on both. Only a downplane is cut away at any altitude.
18. `1qgx7no` First altitude: above 1000 ft you cut away; below only silver (USPA ISP). Then schools teach one of two ways. Learn one clean sequence: which does your school teach?
19. `1rfw90` Pilot chute wrapped or in tow: emergency procedure immediately, by 2500 ft. The AAD is not a plan.
20. `1vxd7dv` 2500 ft: decide where to go and which side the pattern is. 2000 → 1000 ft: wait over the holding point. 900 ft: start downwind. 600 ft: turn to base. 300 ft: turn to final, into the wind. About 10 ft: flare.
21. `1xp4bxq` Never let the toggles back up: the canopy would dive. Hold, fly straight, finish near the ground and be ready for PLF.
22. `39hhji` 5-second rule: 5 s is the upper limit, not waiting time. Not clearly better? Emergency procedure, as a student never below 2500 ft. With a spin or a canopy that clearly can't fly there are no 5 s: act immediately.
23. `5alreq` Loosen the chest strap and unhook the RSL if there's time, half brakes at about 10 ft, deep breath, stay in the harness until you're in the water. Then cut away, swim forward out of the harness, leave the gear.
24. `6fyuro` Altitude is more important than stability: unstable or turning at 5500 ft? Open anyway.
25. `6q10ca` On a roof: collapse the canopy; if the wind drags you, cut away (unhook RSL first if you can).
26. `9ci9yx` Obstacle below 300 ft: half brakes; the smallest flat braked turn that misses it, or go into it feet and knees together, flare and PLF. Never a sharp turn.
27. `csfzg` Cut away the main canopy, at any altitude (unhook the RSL first if there's time). Then steer the reserve and land normally.
28. `jy34w3` A downplane falls fast. It's the only exception to the 1000 ft hard deck: cut away the main regardless of altitude.
29. `kxk63` The AAD fires only very low, at a set altitude, and the RSL is only a backup. Pulling the handles is always your job: pull silver yourself, immediately.
30. `zx50gg` A biplane flies. Keep both, gently steer the front one with its rear risers, don't touch the back one, no flare, PLF. A flare can slow the front canopy and let the back one fly over it.

### Drift found and fixed
- "Hard pull" was rendered as "twarde otwarcie" (= hard *opening*, a different thing) in `tksgxp` and `1v5g8rl`;
  changed to "trudne wyciągnięcie (hard pull)" everywhere.
- "Spin / spinning" was split between "wirowanie/wiruje" and "rotacja"; unified to "rotacja / rotuje".
- "Biplane" had three forms (biplan, dwupłat, dwupłatowiec); unified to "biplan".
- "Hard deck" was sometimes left bare in English; now "twarda granica (hard deck)" throughout.
- "Pilot chute in tow" had three forms; unified to "pilocik na holu (pilot chute in tow)".
- Tutor prompt (`108z8a3`): "Reply in English" became "Odpowiadaj po polsku" on purpose, matching how the app swaps the
  reply language for other languages.

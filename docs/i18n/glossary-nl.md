# Glossary: Dutch (nl), Netherlands and Flanders

Terms as used in Dutch-speaking skydiving (KNVvL afdeling Parachutespringen in the Netherlands; Flemish clubs under
the Belgian federation). Dutch jumpers keep many English words in daily use (cutaway, slider, flare, toggles, line
twists, AAD, RSL, lock-on, hard deck, spot, exit, downwind/base/final, pin check), so the app keeps them and uses
Dutch where a common Dutch word exists (hoofdscherm, reserve, hoogtemeter, noodprocedure, beslissingshoogte,
trekhoogte, achterrisers, remmen, brevet). Informal "je" throughout, like the English "you".

Sources:
- KNVvL Basis Veiligheidsreglement (BVR) 2024, referenced in the KNVvL bulletin VB 2024-01 (BVR art. 403): https://www.paracentrumtexel.nl/wp-content/uploads/VB-2024-01-BVR-2024-aanpassing-medische-verklaring.pdf
- Paracentrum Texel, Nieuwsbrief Veiligheid 2019-4 (Dutch safety newsletter, everyday Dutch skydiving vocabulary): https://paracentrumtexel.nl/wp-content/uploads/Nieuwsbrief-Veiligheid-2019-4.pdf
- KNVvL information sheet on paradropzones (Dutch terms for dropzone, springterrein): https://www.knvvl.nl/uploads/images/default/Informatieblad-Risicos-vliegen-over-lierterreinen-en-door-paradropzones-1.pdf
- KNVvL safety advice on emergency parachutes (Dutch: noodparachute, reservescherm, trekken): https://www.knvvl.nl/uploads/images/zweefvliegen/Veiligheidsadvies-gebruik-van-noodparachutes.pdf
- USPA SIM 4-1 (the English source the app follows): https://uspa.org/sim/4-1
- Parachutist, decision altitude vs cutaway hard deck: https://parachutist.com/p/PgrID/2839/PageID/4/artmid/2830/articleid/9488

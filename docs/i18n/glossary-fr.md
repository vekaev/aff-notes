# French glossary (fr): AFF notes

Audience: French speakers (France, Belgium, Switzerland, Québec) doing an AFF course, here at a USPA dropzone.
Terms follow the FFP (Fédération Française de Parachutisme) where it has one; where French skydivers keep the
English word, it is kept. Register: "tu" and short imperatives, as French schools speak to students.

## Sources
- FFP, Directive Technique 49 (méthodes d'enseignement, brevets), 27 March 2026:
  https://paramag.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Directive-Technique-49-modifiee-27-mars-2026.pdf
  Confirms: "Progression Accompagnée en Chute (PAC)", "déclencheur de sécurité", "poignée témoin, lecture
  altimètre", "élévateurs arrière", "circuit d'approche et technique du posé", "zone de posé", "largage",
  "séparation", "incidents et remèdes", "ouverture : 1 500 mètres minimum".
- FFP safety notice on RSL / Collins lanyard (sac-harnais, RSL, voilure de secours, libération):
  https://paramag.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/IS-2024.0318-–-Controle-de-lintegrite-de-la-sangle-type-4-de-tous-les-sacs-harnais-equipes-de-RSL-et-Collins-Lanyard.pdf

  https://paramag.fr/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/10mars2022_FFP_sécurisation-de-lactivité-1.5.pdf

Drifts checked and fixed during translation:
- "C-1: first release" was first written "premier largage" (first drop); fixed to "premier lâcher" (instructors let go).
- "pull" is "ouvrir/ouverture" for the main and "tirer" for handles; "pull silver" always "tire la poignée argentée", never "ouvre", so the reserve action is not confused with the main deployment.
- "altitude" in emergency numbers is "hauteur" (above ground, FFP usage), consistently.

## For a native instructor to check
1. "AFF" kept with "(PAC)" once, instead of using "PAC" throughout.
2. "Extracteur en traîne" for pilot chute in tow (some say "extracteur en drapeau").
3. "Cercle de conscience" for circle of awareness (no FFP term found).
4. "Hauteur plancher" for hard deck.
5. "Torche" for streamer and "twists" for line twists (vs "vrilles").

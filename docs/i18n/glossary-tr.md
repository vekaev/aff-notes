# Glossary: Turkish (tr), Türkiye

Terms as used in Turkish skydiving training (Türk Hava Kurumu / THK Türkkuşu, Türkiye Havacılık Federasyonu paraşüt
branşı) and at Turkish drop zones (Skydive Fethiye, Efes, İstanbul). Turkish jumpers keep many English words: AFF,
flare, slider, arch, box, lock-on, hard deck, cutaway, PLF, RSL, AAD, rigger, pin check, downwind/base/final,
biplane / side by side / downplane, bag lock, line-over. The app keeps those and adds the Turkish word once where it
helps ("ip burulması", "paraşütçü iniş yuvarlanması (PLF)").

Sources (thin: no public THK glossary or Turkish AFF manual was found online; a native instructor must check this):
- AFF in Turkish ("Hızlandırılmış Serbest Düşüş"), Turkish skydiving blog: https://www.bizevdeyokuz.com/?p=1965
- AFF in Turkish, general term list on a Turkish site: https://www.bizevdeyokuz.com/tag/havada/
- THK course structure (forum, unverified): https://www.kadinlarkulubu.com/konu/skydiving.31707/latest
- English originals for each concept: USPA SIM 4-1 https://www.uspa.org/sim/4-1 and USPA skydive school https://www.uspa.org/skydiveschool/A
- Turkish aviation pattern words (rüzgâraltı/downwind, base, final) follow common Turkish ATC/PPL usage (SHGM PPL material).

Choices worth knowing:
- The AI prompt (`108z8a3`) ends "Türkçe cevap ver." (English said "Reply in English."): localised on purpose.
- Language name `English` kept as-is.
- Feet kept with `ft`, Turkish thousands dot (5.500 ft); percentages written the Turkish way (%22); decimal comma
  (6,2 milyon). "6 in (15 cm)" written "6 inç (15 cm)"; "8 h" written "8 saat".
- "Kat. A" = Category A in "(USPA Kat. A)".
- The exit-count commands (Check in, Prop, Up, Down, Arch) and "OK" are kept in English: they are shouted words.

## Least sure (for a native instructor)

1. **rear risers = "arka askılar"**: some Turkish DZs say "arka riser" or "arka kayışlar".
2. **arch** kept in English; THK may teach "kavis" or "kavis pozisyonu".
3. **PLF = "paraşütçü iniş yuvarlanması"**: THK static-line courses may use a different name for the landing roll.
4. **practice touches = "deneme dokunuşları"** and **circle of awareness = "farkındalık çemberi"**: literal; local
   instructors may use the English words.
5. **cutaway handle = "kesme kolu"** / reserve handle = "yedek açma kolu": common usage, but no official THK source found.

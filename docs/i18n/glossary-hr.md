# Glossary: Croatian (hr)

Terms as used in Croatian parachuting (Hrvatski zrakoplovni savez, padobranska sekcija; Croatian clubs and the
civil aviation rules). Also readable in Bosnia. Croatian skydivers keep many English words (AFF, slider, flare,
cutaway, RSL, AAD, box, lock on, wave-off, downplane, biplane); where they do, the app keeps the English word and
adds the Croatian one in brackets once.

Sources:
- Pravilnik o padobranstvu (NN 65/2015), Croatian parachuting rulebook: definitions of "glavni padobran", "rezervni
  padobran" (used in case of "otkaz glavnog padobrana"), "padobranski automat" (AAD), RSL as "veza između slobodnih
  krajeva glavnog padobrana i sistema za aktiviranje rezervnog padobrana" (so risers = "slobodni krajevi"),
  "doskočište", "učenik padobranac", "skok na slobodno", "nastavnik padobranstva", AFF definition, min. opening
  altitude 900 m for students, ground wind limit 7 m/s for students:
  https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2015_06_65_1252.html
  (also https://www.zakon.hr/c/podzakonski-propis/12608/nn-65-15-od-12.06.2015.-pravilnik-o-padobranstvu)
- Hrvatski zrakoplovni savez (HZS), national FAI member, parachuting commission and sport licences:
  https://www.crocontrol.hr/app/uploads/2020/05/Prezentacija_Hrvatskog_zrakoplovnog_saveza-Compatibility-Mode.pdf
- CCAA (Hrvatska agencija za civilno zrakoplovstvo), parachutist licence forms: https://www.ccaa.hr/
- Croatian aviation traffic-circuit legs (pozadinski / bočni / završni prilaz): standard Croatian ATC and flight-school
  usage.
- USPA SIM glossary (English reference): https://uspa.org/SIM/Glossary

Not found online: a public HZS AFF training manual in Croatian. Terms marked "usage" come from common Croatian
dropzone speech, not a written source, and should be checked by a Croatian instructor.

# Glossario italiano (it) - AFF notes

Terms used by Italian skydiving schools (ENAC-licensed schools, Aero Club d'Italia sport framework) and in the
Italian theory exam question bank. Where Italian dropzones keep the English word, it is kept, with the Italian word
in brackets once where useful.

## Sources
- Italian skydiving exam question bank (quizvds.it), chapters "Procedure in situazioni di emergenza" and
  "Tecnica di utilizzo dei paracadute plananti": https://quizvds.it/it-it/pdf/paracadutismo/procedure-in-situazioni-di-emergenza ,
  https://quizvds.it/it-it/pdf/paracadutismo/tecnica-paracadute-plananti (terms: principale, riserva, sganciare,
  aprire la riserva, pilotino estrattore, throw-out, comandi, sbloccare i comandi, funi di comando, bretelle
  posteriori/anteriori, avvitamento del fascio funicolare, slider, governabilità, separazione, deriva, box position,
  sottovento, finale, punto di lancio, asse di lancio, RSL, AAD/CYPRES, quota).
- ENAC, licenza di paracadutista and safety information on parachuting (AFF levels, "Istruttore AFF"):
  https://www.enac.gov.it/sicurezza-aerea/certificazione-del-personale/paracadutista ,
  https://www.enac.gov.it/sicurezza-aerea/safety-information-attivita-paracadutistica/
- ENAC SI-PARA-001/2026 (AFF level III incident, "sgancio", "riserva"): https://www.enac.gov.it/app/uploads/2026/09/SI-PARA-001_2026.pdf
- Italian AFF school course pages (7 livelli, 2 istruttori livelli 1-3): https://www.emagister.it/corso_di_paracadutismo_aff-ec2351729.htm

### Drift found and fixed
- `1tdx4dn` ("5, 4, 3, 2, 1 is a count: one smooth pull"): first draft said "una richiamata fluida" (one smooth flare), which could read as a whole new flare; changed to "una sola trazione fluida" (one single smooth pull), matching `548vyo`.
- `1meuwzm` (head-on traffic, "the higher canopy has to move out of your way"): first draft "deve toglierti di mezzo" can read as "has to get rid of you"; changed to "deve spostarsi dalla tua traiettoria".
- `a0kd8i` (category E "flips, rolls"): first draft used "avvitamenti" for rolls, which is the same word as line twists; changed to "capriole, tonneau" so "avvitamento" only ever means line twists.
- "Stowed / unstow brakes": kept consistently as "comandi bloccati" / "sbloccare i comandi" (the exam bank's wording), never "freni rilasciati", so "let the toggles back up" ("lasciare risalire i comandi") is not confused with unstowing.
- "Pull" (deploy) is "apri / apertura" everywhere; "pull" of the flare is "trazione"; "pull silver/red" is "tira l'argento / il rosso". Checked that no emergency step changed order.

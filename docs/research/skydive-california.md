Researched 2026-11-16 (web, Reddit via Arctic Shift, YouTube). Read-only; nothing requested from the dropzone.

- Skydive California, LLC ("Skydive Cal", "SDCA", "Tracy"), USPA member DZ since ~2014, own airfield CA74
  (runway 12/30, ~2,063 ft). https://skydivecalifornia.com · info@skydivecalifornia.com · 209-835-7474.
- Social: https://www.facebook.com/SkydiveCalifornia/ · https://www.instagram.com/skydiveca/ ·

## Useful pages
- https://skydivecalifornia.com/learn-to-skydive/ — AFF overview: USPA ISP, categories A–E, 4–6 h ground school,
  A license = 25 jumps + written/oral + check dive.
- https://skydivecalifornia.com/blog/learn-skydiving-at-skydive-california-aff-training-guide/ — best page: ground
  school topics, each category, exit 13,000 ft, Cat A deployment 5,500 ft, radio help under canopy.
- https://skydivecalifornia.com/blog/plf-parachute-landing-fall/ — PLF explainer.
- https://skydivecalifornia.com/experienced/ — AAD required; flying the wrong landing pattern repeatedly = suspension.
- Categories: B = 90° turns and forward movement; C-1 first release; D-1/D-2 180°/360° turns; E flips, barrel rolls, tracking.

## What jumpers say (Reddit, paraphrased)
- Thorough, safety-focused AFF, good instructors, well-kept student gear; open Fri–Sun all year; often less windy than Byron.
- One side of the landing area is farmland you must not land in; flat farm fields everywhere, get help spotting early, fly a proper pattern.
- Threads: https://www.reddit.com/r/SkyDiving/comments/1acw1ux/ · /n0ogvr/ · /b3xk0z/

## Not public
No student manual, briefing PDF, landing/holding-area map, wind or turn limits, decision/hard-deck altitudes,
canopy pattern altitudes. Those come from the DZ briefing — the reason to ask them for the ground-school slides.

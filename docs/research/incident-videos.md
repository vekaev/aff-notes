# Skydiving mistakes on video — clips for the "what not to do" section

Researched 2026-11-16. Read-only: YouTube metadata, descriptions, captions and storyboard thumbnails
(`yt-dlp --skip-download`), JOINTHETEEM write-ups on jointheteem.com. No video files were downloaded.
All picks are non-fatal; none shows blood or an injury close-up in the frames I checked.

## How the times were checked

- **Storyboard** — I looked at YouTube's own preview thumbnails (one frame every ~1 s, or every ~2 s on
  longer videos) and found the frames where the mistake happens. Accurate to about ±1 s (±2 s where noted).
  This also is how I checked "non-graphic": I saw every thumbnail of the window, not every video frame.
- **Captions** — matched to the spoken words in the video.
- **Heatmap only** — YouTube's "most replayed" peak; I did not see the frames. Marked *unverified*.
- `embed` = ready-to-use link: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/<id>?start=<s>&end=<e>`.
- JOINTHETEEM videos open with a ~5 s "Friday Freakout" title card, and many replay the incident in slow
  motion after the real-time part; the windows below point at the real-time part unless noted.

## 1. Low turns / hook turns near the ground

### 1.1 Low turn into the ground, broken femur and ankle (best pick)
- **ID** `qiJUPUghXyc` — "Friday Freakout: Skydiver's Low Turn + Broken Bones = Hard Lesson Learned" — JOINTHETEEM — 54 s
- **Window 34–43 s** (Storyboard): toggle turn toward the pond at ~32 s, canopy still diving at ~40 s, ground impact
  ~41–43 s. A first, shorter cut of the same turn is at 26–30 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — the impact is a blur of grass and sky, then the canopy on the grass. Injuries are only told in text.
- **What went wrong:** a jumper with 212 jumps tried a downwind swoop over the pond, started the turn too low and
  could not get out of the dive before the ground. Lesson: no big or low turns before you are trained for them; under
  ~300 ft (and lower still for students) only small flat turns.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-skydivers-low-turn-broken-bones-hard-lesson-learned/)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/qiJUPUghXyc?start=34&end=43`

### 1.2 Turn started too low, stays in the dive, bounces
- **ID** `NGxkimvfnb8` — "Friday Freakout: Swoop Fail, Skydiver Bounces On Hard Landing & Breaks Elbow" — JOINTHETEEM — 105 s
- **Window 39–48 s** (Storyboard): dive at ~41–44 s, legs fly up on the bounce at ~45.6 s, ground at ~46.6 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — camera view of grass, sky and legs; no injury shown.
- **What went wrong:** the wind had turned 180°, he was too low for his planned turn, did a shorter one anyway and stayed
  in the dive too long; he hit, bounced, and broke his elbow landing on a straight arm. Lesson: if the plan falls apart, fly a
  normal pattern and land; never "send it" low.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-swoop-fail-skydiver-bounces-on-hard-landing-breaks-elbow/)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/NGxkimvfnb8?start=39&end=48`

### 1.3 Swoop turn too low, lands in the pond (ground camera)
- **ID** `5Lj_O4MLo0M` — "Skydive swoop crash, what not to do" — Chris Forman — 31 s
- **Window 20–29 s** (Storyboard): canopy turning and diving from ~19–22 s, hits the pond ~26 s with a big splash.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — seen from the ground, ends in a splash; he walked away, sore for a month (per the uploader).
- **What went wrong:** in the jumper's own words, "what not to do when you are low": the turn was too low to recover
  before the water. Lesson: the same as 1.1, seen from outside — the canopy dives hard after a turn.
- **Source:** [uploader's description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Lj_O4MLo0M)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/5Lj_O4MLo0M?start=20&end=29`

Extra: `D7jjF8aS0qU` "Friday Freakout: Low Hook Turn, Bounce, Broken Ankle" (192 s) — low hook and bounce at about
**86–96 s** (Storyboard, ±2 s); the rest is the jumper packing up. Short write-up only ("don't teach yourself to swoop"),
[YouTube description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7jjF8aS0qU).

## 2. Flare errors

### 2.1 Flared too high — and the right fix: hold, then finish (best pick)
- **ID** `hZXQpFu8I1s` — "AFF Student Landings Flared Too High Skydive City Z-Hills" — Zach Foreman (AFF instructor) — 17 s
- **Window 2–10 s** (Captions): radio says "get ready, one" at ~3 s, the student flares too early, instructor says
  "hold it, hold it…" (3–6 s), then "finish, finish" (6.5–8.5 s).
- **Non-graphic:** yes — soft landing far from the camera. Note: the canopy is small in frame; the radio audio carries it.
- **What went wrong:** the student started the flare too high and planed out above the ground. Lesson: if you flared high,
  don't let the toggles back up — hold them where they are and finish the flare when you are at the right height.
- **Source:** [instructor's description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZXQpFu8I1s)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/hZXQpFu8I1s?start=2&end=10`

### 2.2 AFF student, flare timing wrong, hard landing and tumble
- **ID** `N8vpe-olEYs` — "Skydive Hard Landing AFF B @ Ellington, CT Connecticut Parachutists" — jpelligrinelli — 32 s
- **Window 9–17 s** (Storyboard): student canopy comes in fast at ~10–13 s, hits the ground ~14.5 s and tumbles over.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — ground camera, wide shot; the student gets up.
- **What went wrong:** the uploader says only "Too fast of a flare" — the flare was a quick stab instead of a smooth, full
  pull at the right height, so the canopy still had speed at touchdown. Lesson: flare smoothly and all the way, feet and knees

- **Source:** [uploader's description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N8vpe-olEYs)

## 3. Downwind landing / turbulence

### 3.1 Sudden 35-knot wind and turbulence, lands crosswind and is dragged (best pick)
- **ID** `1h7fvb46wug` — "Friday Freakout: Scared Skydiver Lands Parachute Backwards In High Winds" — JOINTHETEEM — 119 s
- **Window 54–63 s** (Storyboard + heatmap peak 55–61 s): last approach at ~50–58 s, touchdown ~59.5 s, tumble and drag 60–63 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — canopy and grass, he is dragged but no injury shown.
- **What went wrong:** weather turned while the load was in the air; he panicked, flew crosswind instead of facing into the
  wind, so the landing was rough and the canopy dragged him. Lesson: in strong wind face straight into it for landing, then
  collapse the canopy (pull one toggle / a steering line) right away.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-scared-skydiver-lands-parachute-backwards-in-high-winds/)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/1h7fvb46wug?start=54&end=63`

### 3.2 Downwind landing, legs collapse
- **ID** `tG63N8Y-ti4` — "Skydiving accident on landing" — Paul Lewis — 30 s
- **Window 11–21 s** (Storyboard, low-res thumbnails): fast downwind approach, impact ~18–20 s, canopy on the ground after ~24 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — helmet-camera view; no broken bones per the jumper.
- **What went wrong:** the landing "T" was set the wrong way; he noticed at 300 ft but with 19 canopies around he chose to land
  downwind, could not run off the ground speed and his legs folded. Lesson: a downwind landing is much faster — if you must, flare
  fully and do a PLF instead of trying to run it out; check the windsock yourself, not only the T.
- **Source:** [jumper's own description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tG63N8Y-ti4)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/tG63N8Y-ti4?start=11&end=21`

### 3.3 Dust devil re-inflates the canopy and drags the jumper
- **ID** `x6-xVbNCE5Y` — "Friday Freakout: Skydiver Dragged Across Ground By Dust Devil!" — JOINTHETEEM — 42 s
- **Window 20–29 s** (Storyboard): touchdown ~18–20 s, canopy fills sideways ~21–22 s, dragged 23–29 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — dust and canopy; he is not shown hurt.
- **What went wrong:** a dust devil (whirlwind) hit right after landing at Skydive Arizona and re-inflated a big canopy (Safire 210).
  Lesson: watch for dust devils on hot days; if one drags you, collapse the canopy with a toggle/steering line, or (if trained)
  disconnect the RSL and cut away.
- **Source:** [JOINTHETEEM description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x6-xVbNCE5Y)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/x6-xVbNCE5Y?start=20&end=29`

## 4. Obstacle landings

### 4.1 Power lines (best pick)
- **ID** `KWO7U-PNEus` — "Friday Freakout: Skydiver Hits Power Lines, Parachute Goes Boom!" — JOINTHETEEM — 53 s
- **Window 20–28 s** (Storyboard): heads for a gap in the trees at ~20–23 s, lines appear ~24 s, canopy hits them ~25 s,
  hanging next to the trees 26–28 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — canopy, wires and trees; he is not shown hurt.
- **What went wrong:** on an off-DZ landing he overshot, aimed for a gap in the trees and did not see the power lines along the
  driveway. Lesson: power lines run along roads, driveways and straight cuts through trees — look for the poles; avoid them early,
  and if you can't, land in trees or water instead. If you will hit them: drop anything in your hands, half-brakes, feet and knees
  tight, touch only one wire, and don't touch anyone until the power is off.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-skydiver-hits-power-lines-parachute-goes-boom/) (quotes the USPA SIM)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/KWO7U-PNEus?start=20&end=28`

### 4.2 Trees, after a big low turn and target fixation
- **ID** `QkUIomzFj_c` — "Friday Freakout: Skydiver Flies Straight Into Trees, Breaks Ankle" — JOINTHETEEM — 167 s
- **Window 90–100 s** (Storyboard, ±2 s): low over the field ~90–94 s, trees at ~96 s, in the branches ~98–100 s.
  Slow-motion replay of the same at ~118–148 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — branches and leaves; the broken ankle is only told in text.
- **What went wrong:** downwind leg started late, then a long 180° toggle turn pointed him at the trees, and at the last second he
  steered *into* them although open space was to his left (target fixation). Lesson: fly a normal pattern with 90° turns, and look
  at where you want to go, not at the obstacle.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-skydiver-flies-straight-into-trees-breaks-ankle/)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/QkUIomzFj_c?start=90&end=100`

### 4.3 Rooftop and power lines on a late off-DZ decision
- **ID** `cxnjXmlNqaw` — "Friday Freakout: Skydiver Skims Power Lines and Rooftop on Tight Off DZ Landing" — JOINTHETEEM — 144 s
- **Window 110–120 s** (Storyboard, ±2 s): houses below ~110–112 s, roof right under his feet ~114–116 s, on the ground ~118 s.
  A replay follows at ~124–134 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** nobody checked the spot, the group exited far from the DZ; he tried to get back, then chose a landing
  area too late and ended up between a roof, power lines and a tree, lifting his legs to clear the roof. His own words: he should have
  decided earlier, with more altitude. Lesson: check the spot; if you can't make the DZ, pick a safe field high (by ~2,000 ft) and commit.
- **Source:** [JOINTHETEEM description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cxnjXmlNqaw)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/cxnjXmlNqaw?start=110&end=120`

### 4.4 River (water landing)
- **ID** `5Sh4PouHIEQ` — "Friday Freakout: Skydiver Has "Off DZ" Landing... Into A River." — JOINTHETEEM — 131 s
- **Window 40–49 s** (Storyboard, ±2 s; heatmap peak 31–42 s): river between the trees ~39–41 s, water close 43–47 s,
  lands in the water ~49 s; he stands up in waist-deep water after.
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** winds picked up after takeoff; he ended up downwind and short of the DZ, kept hoping to make it back, and
  by the time he looked for an out the only option was the river. Lesson: when you see you are not getting back, pick a field early

## 5. Canopy collisions / near-misses in the pattern

### 5.1 Collision at 250 ft in a crowded pattern (best pick)
- **ID** `BUogil8WvRI` — "Friday Freakout: Crazy Canopy Collision at 250 Feet!" — JOINTHETEEM — 90 s (vertical video)
- **Window 16–24 s** (Storyboard): yellow canopy turns into his path ~18–19 s, contact ~20 s, spun around 21–24 s.
  Slow-motion replay from ~29 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes — both landed; one had minor line burns.
- **What went wrong:** four jumpers on final at once; one did a front-riser 90° turn and the other, who thought it was farther
  away, flew into it. Lesson: keep your eyes on traffic, give others lots of room, don't make sudden turns near the ground, and on busy
  days spread out in the sky and in the landing area.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-crazy-canopy-collision-at-250-feet/)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/BUogil8WvRI?start=16&end=24`

### 5.2 Collision at 100 ft after an S-turn on final
- **ID** `P-4N_cn3ZA0` — "Friday Freakout: Crazy Canopy Collision at 100 Feet!" — JOINTHETEEM — 62 s
- **Window 13–21 s** (Storyboard + heatmap peak 16–19 s): turquoise canopy below him ~15–17 s, contact ~17.7 s, spinning after.
  Replay at ~36–41 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes in the frames — but she broke a leg (told in the text), so a softer pick is 5.1.
- **What went wrong:** he flew a left-hand pattern; she made an S-turn on final into his path, and her canopy collapsed at 100 ft.

## 6. AFF student mistakes in freefall

### 6.1 Unstable at pull time, almost forgets to pull (best pick)
- **ID** `jA8A3sv7hx8` — "Worst Skydiving Student Mistake Spinning Low Altitude Deployment AFF Category E2 AFF Happens" — Zach Foreman (AFF instructor) — 106 s
- **Window 86–96 s** (Storyboard + heatmap peak 91–96 s): student head-high and turning at ~82–88 s, still not pulled while the
  instructor watches, instructor breaks away ~95–97 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** last jump before solo; head high, legs on his butt, unstable — and he nearly forgot to pull. Lesson: the
  order is 1) pull, 2) pull at the right altitude, 3) be stable while pulling. Stability is the least important of the three.
- **Source:** [instructor's description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jA8A3sv7hx8)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/jA8A3sv7hx8?start=86&end=96`

### 6.2 Can't find the pilot-chute handle, instructor deploys for him
- **ID** `qidpLSdggOo` — "Skydiver can't find parachute handle Category D1 No Pull AFF Happens" — Zach Foreman — 70 s
- **Window 47–56 s** (Storyboard): instructor docks on the student ~46–48 s, holds him while he searches, throws the student's
  pilot chute ~54–55 s, canopy deploys ~56 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** the student kept overshooting his turns (no "start – coast – stop") and, worst, stopped arching when he reached
  back, so the handle moved up his back and he couldn't find it. Lesson: keep the arch while you reach; if you can't find it after two tries,
  go to your reserve plan (and your instructor will pull for you).
- **Source:** [instructor's description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qidpLSdggOo)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/qidpLSdggOo?start=47&end=56`

### 6.3 Student flips and spins away from the instructor
- **ID** `n3pSBH7bdDc` — "Friday Freakout: Skydive Student Spins Wildly Out of Control" — JOINTHETEEM — 182 s
- **Window 23–33 s** (Storyboard, ±2 s): student flips over at ~24–30 s and spins away; the instructor loses him by ~32 s.
  Slow-motion replay from ~71 s. He deploys at ~67 s (far away).
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** on an AFF "level 6" jump the student went into a flat spin; the instructor could not stop it and lost him. The
  student still deployed and landed fine. Lesson: in a spin, go back to a neutral, symmetrical arch (look at the horizon, relax) and never
  lose altitude awareness — pull on time even if unstable. The write-up also questions whether he was ready for that level.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-skydive-student-spins-wildly-out-of-control/)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/n3pSBH7bdDc?start=23&end=33`

## 7. Cutaways from real malfunctions (good outcome)

### 7.1 Spinning line-over → cutaway → RSL opens reserve (best pick)
- **ID** `wx1gJRlAdPE` — "Friday Freakout: Line-Over or Mystery Malfunction?" — JOINTHETEEM — 94 s
- **Window 23–33 s** (Storyboard): main spinning 20–26 s, cutaway ~27 s (sky, then ground), white reserve opening ~31–33 s.
  Slow-motion replay from ~38 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** stable deployment, but the main started spinning (most likely a line-over from packing). He tried the risers, felt the
  G-force rising and cut away; the RSL pulled the reserve before he reached the handle. Lesson: a spinning malfunction gets worse fast —
  decide, cut away, pull reserve; and pack carefully, without rushing or chatting.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-line-over-or-mystery-malfunction/)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/wx1gJRlAdPE?start=23&end=33`

### 7.2 Line twists + line-over, cutaway at decision altitude
- **ID** `gksZrqXDIPU` — "Friday Freakout: Skydiver Has Double Malfunction With Line Twists & Line Over" — JOINTHETEEM — 183 s
- **Window 68–78 s** (Storyboard ±2 s + heatmap peak 64–74 s): still fighting the line-over at ~68–72 s, main gone ~75 s, short freefall;
  yellow reserve open by ~85 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** first cutaway for this jumper: he kicked out of the line twists but could not clear the line-over, and cut away at his
  hard deck as planned (then landed downwind). Lesson: work a malfunction only down to your decision altitude, then follow the procedure.
- **Source:** [JOINTHETEEM description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gksZrqXDIPU)
- embed: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/gksZrqXDIPU?start=68&end=78`

### 7.3 Spinning main, stuck cutaway cable, reserve + downplane
- **ID** `cN0Rco_u_2Q` — "Friday Freakout: Scary Spinning Downplane, Double Parachute Malfunction" — JOINTHETEEM — 192 s
- **Window 32–42 s** (Storyboard ±2 s): spinning main, hand goes to the reserve handle ~33 s, orange reserve opens with line twists
  next to the main 37–42 s. The downplane and main cutaway follow at ~45–57 s; landing ~81 s.
- **Non-graphic:** yes.
- **What went wrong:** a tension knot turned into a spinning malfunction; the cutaway cable stuck, so — losing altitude — he pulled the reserve;
  both canopies went into a downplane until he got the main away. Lesson: keep your gear clean and maintained (a dirty cable housing may have
  jammed it), and know the two-canopies-out procedures. Advanced; better for later study than for a first look.
- **Source:** [jointheteem.com write-up](https://jointheteem.com/videos/friday-freakout-skydiving/friday-freakout-scary-spinning-downplane-double-parachute-malfunction/)

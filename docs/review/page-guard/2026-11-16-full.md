## Readability notes

- The rig photos match their captions and sit under the drawing as asked. The leg-strap photo shows only the leg straps
  while the part is "Chest and leg straps".
- Problem 1 is the most important for a student reading on a phone at the dropzone: the key numbers (6,000 lock on,
  5,500 pull, 2,500 decide, 1,000 hard deck) show up most often without "ft" exactly where they are biggest.

# Expert review: first-screen-dropzone   (2026-11-16; `src/components/Hero.tsx` (first screen: jump picture, "numbers to know by heart", "If the main is bad", timers), `src/content/dropzone.ts` (Part V: "Skydive California", "Read and watch next"))

## Summary
The first screen's numbers are mostly right and match USPA Category A: 13,000 exit (SDCA), 6,000 lock on, 5,500 pull, 2,500 decision, 1,000 hard deck, 900/600/300 pattern, 3 s altitude checks. Two lines on that screen need fixing before a first jump, because the student will memorise exactly those lines:
1. The 1,000 ft line ("no cutaway below") leaves out the downplane exception that Part IV and USPA both teach.
2. The "5 s count after the pull" does not match USPA Category A, which says count to three and then look. Which count this school teaches has to come from the instructors.

Part V is mostly backed by the dropzone's own pages. The weak spots:
- "Wind usually from the north" has no source.
- "One whole side is farmland: don't land there" could push a student who is long into a bad off-field decision.
- The suspension rule is credited to "jumpers say", when it is the dropzone's own published rule.
- Two reading-list descriptions don't match the articles they link to.

## Missing
- **Downplane exception on the first screen** (finding 1). The one exception to a by-heart rule has to sit next to the rule. Source: USPA Category A, emergency procedures; SIM 4-1.
- **Canopy check words on the first screen.** "Is it there? Is it square? Is it steerable?" (USPA Category A), with the controllability check: flare, then turn right and left.
- **"Pull by 5,500 even if unstable; if in doubt, pull."** The by-heart list gives pull altitude but not the rule that altitude beats stability. Part II has it (part2.ts:390), and USPA Category A ties the pull to altitude (wave off and pull at 5,500). One line on the first screen would close the gap.
- **No-radio plan.** Part V says "Radio help under canopy" (SDCA training guide: "Radio-assisted parachute guidance for landing") but not what to do if the radio is silent or wrong. Ask the instructors: "If my radio fails, do I just fly the planned pattern? Any signal from the ground?"
- **Student wind limit.** BSR (SIM 2-1 H.1.1): 14 mph for solo students on ram-air. Ask whether Skydive California uses a lower limit for Category A.
- **Landing-area layout.** The page rightly says to ask for the ground-school slides. It should also say to walk the landing area, holding point and windsock spot with an instructor before the first jump.

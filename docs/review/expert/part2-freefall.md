# Expert review: Part II "Freefall"   (2026-11-16; `src/content/part2.ts`, chapters body, flow, altitude, signals; the `Altimeter` drawing in `src/widgets.tsx`; numbers cross-checked with `src/components/Hero.tsx`, `part3.ts`, `part4.ts` and `dropzone.ts`)

## Summary
I trust this part on the numbers that matter. Exit is at 13,000, lock on at 6,000, pull at 5,500 on Category A, altitude checks every 3 s, three practice touches, and "lost one instructor, carry on; lost both, pull now". All of these match the USPA SIM Category A page, and the first screen, Part III and the dropzone chapter give the same figures. Most of the hand-signal cards match the credited Skydive Fundamentals videos frame by frame.

The worst problem is the "Overloaded or lost? ... pull at 5,500" default and its quiz. Its wrong options read "lost" as "lost the instructors", so the page teaches waiting until 5,500 in exactly the case where USPA says pull immediately. It also never says "already lower: pull now".

Other problems:
- Part II never states the USPA pull priorities ("pull at your altitude, stable or not"; "your instructor pulls: you pull").
- The lock-on card explains the 1,000 ft wrongly.
- The wave-off is hedged "if your school teaches it", but it is the USPA standard.
- The circle-of-awareness order differs from USPA and from the app's own clip.

## Missing
- **"Your instructor pulls: you pull"**, and pull priorities (pull at your altitude, stable or not). See finding 2. Source: uspa.org/skydiveschool/A.
- **Two USPA Category A signals are not among the cards**: **Open hand = release the pilot chute** (used if a student holds the hackey after the throw) and **Legs in = retract legs slightly** (backsliding). Source: uspa.org/skydiveschool/A, hand-signal list.
- **Can't find the handle on a practice touch**: pause with your hand where you think it is, and the instructor moves it there. Stay relaxed so they can (Skydive Fundamentals, LpoztnjW5c8 at 11:40 and 13:28). USPA: "pause to feel the deployment handle each time".
- **How long the freefall is**: about 5 s per 1,000 ft at terminal, so 13,000 to 5,500 is roughly 45 s, and lock on to pull is about 3 s (Skydive Fundamentals o6hGuKffq1k at 17:46; USPA's ~1,000 ft wave-off sequence). This shows why the dive flow must be finished early.
- **What a wave-off is**: see finding 4.

## Checked and fine
I checked about 45 claims and found them correct. They include:
- **Body position:** exit "hips into the wind, chin up", then belly to earth with the hips lowest; knees at shoulder width, ~45°, toes pointed; arms 90/90; a stiff body is the most common Cat A fault (Skydive Fundamentals).
- **Dive flow:** three practice touches (left hand overhead, right hand to the pilot chute), then a second COA; left (reserve side) before right.
- **Altitude:** check every 3 s and on the wrist signal; lock on at 6,000; pull at 5,500 on Cat A ("ask about later categories" fits USPA, which recommends 4,500 for AFF from Cat B); lost one instructor carry on, lost both pull immediately (ES, UK and RU keep the meaning).
- **Signals:** pull = one finger in any direction; thumb down = arch; V = legs out; the shaking hand; thumbs up; thumb and pinky L = 90/90; bent flat hand = toe taps; fist = practice touch; chin tap; shaking on exit = more hips and chin (Skydive Fundamentals, o6hGuKffq1k at 12:05).
- **Quizzes:** every "right" answer except the COA order (finding 5) is correct, and no wrong option is actually right.
- **Altimeter drawing:** 12,000 ft per turn, colour only low on the dial.
- **Numbers across the app:** 13,000 / 6,000 / 5,500 / 3 s are the same in Hero.tsx and dropzone.ts.
- **Clips:** the titles and channels match the credits (YouTube oEmbed). The transcripts confirm the wall drill at 0:19, the COA at 3:53, the practice touches at 4:34 and the tunnel coaching cues at 2:09.

Main sources: USPA SIM Category A online ground school (uspa.org/skydiveschool/A), USPA Category B page, and the Skydive Fundamentals video transcripts (V4rn53L4isw, o6hGuKffq1k, LpoztnjW5c8, 7OT3X9lVt8c).
